Hello! I really have enjoyed this semester so far. Podcasting has been something that I have been interested in for a while now, so I have enjoyed learning what all goes into creating one. I think that “what’s working” now is that I am learning how to better edit a podcast, plan what I am going to say within my episodes, and I am overall getting more comfortable/ confident within my episodes. Something that I feel is challenging right now is some of the technical sides of editing. Specifically, compression and equalization. I think that I could use more help/ a refresher in those areas. I think that all the assignments in this class thus far have been useful, I particularly have enjoyed how we are tasked with different things for each episode. Also, I love reading about podcasting for week’s where we have a reading. Overall, I have loved this semester in this class!
