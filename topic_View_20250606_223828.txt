After this week's reading, I got a lot of good ideas on how and where to implement sounds into my podcast to amplify the mood that I am talking about and want to portray. I was thinking that whenever I talk about ominous scenes in the movie and I'm interpreting them I can add more ominous lower tones for that part. Another idea I had was to have different sounds for each section, like when I am just giving a summary of the movie for the first third then I can keep the tone kind of light, or depending on the tone of the movie I can match it to that. The second section is usually talks about the different parts of the movie, like sound, casting, acting, and directorial style, so I can definitely keep this music more light-hearted and chill and maybe even add some soundbites from the movie that I think would help viewers get the vibe. In the 3rd section, I usually analyze the movie because A24 makes movies that are not easy to interpret and usually they leave the movie up for the viewers to interpret, I think for this section I will add a lot of thought-provoking music.
