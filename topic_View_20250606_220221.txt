One type of podcast that I rarely ever listen to is a true crime/mystery podcast. I listened to the Podcast S-Town on New York Times. It is a mystery podcast about a man named John B. McLemore from the middle of nowhere Alabama who called into Brian Reed's radio show to report crime and possible murder happening in his town. The story follows Brian as he goes into John's hometown, Woodstock, Alabama. John is a very intelligent but frustrated man. He often discussed how disappointed he was with the wickedness of the world and specifically his hometown which he called "a shit town" hence the podcast name. I was on the edge of my seat listening to all the episodes of this podcast. The story went a completely different direction than I would have expected and there were so many more things I learned about John instead of the murder he called in about. I very much enjoyed this podcast and am planning on listening to more mystery and crime podcasts in the future. I can imagine the production of this podcast must have taken a very long time. The recording of interviews themselves took months of time as the story unfolded. Not to mention the editing and voice overs and opinions that Brian added in after. The extra thoughts and fill in background information is what made this story so interesting to me. Brian's personality really shined through with the way he gave opinions and made witty comments on the interviews he had. I think he prepared by simply showing up but then as the story unfolded he did extra research and digging into medical files and records to get more information to unfold the story. I loved this podcast and am very interested in listening to another one like it soon.
