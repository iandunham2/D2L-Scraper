A type of podcast I never typically find myself listening to is "sports commentary". I am not a huge sports fan to begin with, so it is not something I will go out of my way to listen to. The only time I may hear sports commentary podcasts are through seeing "New Heights" podcast clips on social media but most of the time in the clips I see, they are not discussing sports at that particular moment. 
The sports commentary episode I decided to listen to was titled "Travis on the Super Bowl Loss, Jason on Mixed Emotions and Who's Going Over a Cliff?" on the "New Heights" podcast hosted by Jason and Travis Kelce. To be honest, I enjoyed this episode. Travis Kelce talked about how he was dealing with his emotions regarding his team's recent Super Bowl loss and his thoughts on the entire game. Meanwhile, Jason talked about how he had mixed emotions regarding the game because he really cares for his brother Travis and wanted him to win but, he is also a former Eagles player so, he was happy to see his former teammates succeed as well. I enjoyed how both of these men really talked about their emotions and were not afraid to do so. Personally, I do not see many men especially in the media, discussing their emotions in such a raw, unedited way like these two did in this episode. So, it was quite refreshing to see how honest and open they both were being regarding such a tough situation to talk about. Moreover, I enjoyed hearing both of their commentary on the super bowl game because as I watching it I never really thought about it in such technical terms like typical football players or even fans would. For them to prepare for this type of episode, it most likely took a lot of courage because they were discussing a very emotional topic for both of them. As for the production process, it seems as if both of the brothers have home offices/studios where they go in to film each podcast episode and they have an editor they hire to edit their episodes.
