My podcast originally started by mainly talking about serial killers and lot of the details that tend to be left out. This is in regards to childhood, details of the crime scene and more. While the direction has slightly changed overtime, it still remains the same at its core. A podcast to inform people about serial killers that isn't afraid to get into the darker details that make some people sick to their stomachs. To wrap up this season, I want to look deeper into the psychological lens behind serial killers and draw some commonalities between my past episodes. This is to better allow the audience to depict and look into the mind of a serial killer and it will allow me to go further in depth into the twisted minds some people have. I haven't decided if I want to dedicate the entire episode to analyzing killer's backgrounds or dedicate it to a specific killer as a sendoff. The music choices and editing will likely stay consistent to my prior episodes as I would like to keep as much consistency as possible wherever I can while also still meeting the guideline requirements. I likely will edit and work on my podcast here soon due to keeping quality up before I begin traveling.
