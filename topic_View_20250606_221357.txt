For this episode of the podcast I plan on utilizing Galileo, Library resources from the Kennesaw's databases, and YouTube for the video needed. Usually people that talk about conspiracy theories have videos about it on TikTok, so other social media outlets could be helpful when finding resources on certain topics. I don't really feel it necessary to find credible source for my topic because my podcasts are about conspiracy theories. They are theories, which means they have not been proven or disproven as fact. I feel that "credible" sources mean fact, which is why my resources will be used as such. My topic this week will be about the Mandela Effect. A Mandela Effect is a symbol or occurrence you remember to be something totally different than what is the actual reality. I know a lot about this subject and have experienced it head-on.  I do think it would be useful to find credible sources that have documented the Mandela Effect, and the definition of the conspiracy theory. I plan on using the resources to identify the conspiracy theory and recall recorded occurrences of it taking effect. I think the rest would basically be me explaining how it has effected me, and how it occurs in everyday life in the past and present.
