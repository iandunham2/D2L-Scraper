For this discussion board I dove into different podcast genre's. I do usually only listen to crime and conspiracy podcasts, so I am very used to that type of genre. When searching for new genres I came across a type of podcast that were all the rage a few years ago, and very popular now, ASMRs or "dramatized content". I honestly don't really like ASMR's because all the whispering just makes me uncomfortable. I also don't like chewing too much, but I like seeing what people eat and how much people buy for these videos. I think the production process is the same set up as we would do for a regular episode, but they have to go buy food instead of research like we do for our episodes. They had to go buy whatever food they focus on for the video, and set it up to look appetizing and pretty for their audience One person I saw did it without any food, so I found that interesting. I think they also have to kind of starve themselves to be able to eat the monstrous amount of food most people on these types of podcasts eat. The podcast episode I found is of junk food and healthy food, so it isn't as extravagant as other podcaster with lots of foods. There are two women with just a plate fruit and veggies, and a guy with a sandwich and some sides. I think the idea of this genre of podcast is really cool! These ASMR/mukbang podcasts were known to be of use to people to watch while they eat, so they don't feel lonely. Another reason was that certain types have calming affects and lots of people use them to fall asleep at night. I really like the concept of these podcast not only being entertainment, but also helping others.
Video-  Junk Food vs. Healthy Food: ASMR Eating Sounds with Mississippi Vegan- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SEip0NSKU-4
