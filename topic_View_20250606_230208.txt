When it comes to the idea of making a career out of the life of a podcaster I can only really think of the McElroy Family again. Originally they had a very few collection of podcasts, with one such example being my brother my brother and me. What started out as a simple podcast hosted by a man and his two younger brothers provided the opportunity for the entire family to start a career in podcasting by creating, producing, and staring in a wide variety of shows. Not only does this create a career oppurtinity for the previously mentioned Mcelroys but also for the people who work for them. because just like movies and TV shows, as the content they produce become more and more detailed and reach a higher quality than before it goes without saying that more and more people will be required to maintain and work on the podcast shows in question. From editors that help cut the recorded audio into digestible bits to be published to the various kinds of music artisit that are comissioned to create or aid in the creation of certain music tracks that are exclusive and unique to a given show such as their DND actual play podcast "The Adventure Zone". I also do believe that there are also careers that can be found in making some mediums into podcast. An example to this that comes to mind is diminsion 20 where they play various forms of table top role playing games but it i shot like a show. However the channel in which this form of media lives in, Dropout TV also are known in making these episodes into an audio format that can then be streamed on platforms such as spotify which is actually how I found it.
