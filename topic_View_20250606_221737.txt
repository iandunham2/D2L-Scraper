For my fourth episode, I'm going to be reviewing "Cabrini" by Angel Studios, so I'm going to do some research into the film and its historical background. For the academic source, I'll look at a peer reviewed article about Frances Xavier Cabrini through the Kennesaw State University database. If I don't find something about her specifically, I can look at things about how a saint is canonized or about child poverty in the late 1800s, which was her ministry. For the journalistic source, I could look at reception for the film or perhaps a journalistic article about Saint Cabrini from a reputable news source. For the audio source, I could look at a cast or crew interview about the film or a clip from a historian or religious figure about Saint Cabrini on the Angel Studios YouTube channel. 

All of this is pretty easy to integrate into the episode. The academic source, I'll mention in the background information about the film. For the journalistic source, I could mention about the public reception of the film. For the audio source, I could integrate into the middle or the end section. This is basically like doing a written review but for audio, so I should be able to include all the sources organically.
