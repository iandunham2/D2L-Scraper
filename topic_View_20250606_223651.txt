The idea of adding a soundboard is not new to me, but does seem a little complicated to import into use when trying to create podcasts. When I say soundboard, I am talking about some sort of interface that is loaded with preset sound effects that pop with the click of the button. That is what I think of when someone is implementing sound effects into whatever form of content they are creating. Now, for my podcast as it is sports based I could of course implement crowd roars or crowd boos when I am stating something I do not like or find crazy. Possibly my introduction music could contain more sound effects than just music like the buzzer that sounds at the end of the game or halftimes to signal the end or start of a break. The idea would be to immerse the listener in a more complete experience that feels professional and multi-faceted. Overall, interested in adding sound effects into my podcast but I do not want it to extend or force my to implement aspects that might not have been necessary. Sports podcast does offer a lot of room for different types of sound effects so I will not be short of options.
