My podcast has somewhat shifted directions since the first episode. I began with the intentions of doin conspiracy theories about anything and everything. Now, I am more focused on film and tv conspiracies, and it's been a lot more enjoyable. My final episode will be Harry Potter conspiracy theories. The movies are iconic and have potential to track a larger audience to my podcast. I want to use more sound effects this episode. There is a lot less stress because I am familiar with the tools that we learned to use throughout the semester. I feel really confident that this episode will be my best.
With all of that being said, I do not plan to integrate in the episode and post on Youtube. I think it would complicate the production process for me. There was a point in time where I thought having my own podcast would be cool. I never really want to host a podcast again, but I have a lot of respect for all the podcasters out there. I do not enjoy recording as much as I expected to. However, I surprisingly enjoy the editing process. Cutting clips and adding sound effects makes the greatest different.
