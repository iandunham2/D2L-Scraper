While doing this discussion I learned the majority of the podcast that I watch or listen to don't really use a lot of music. However, On Purpose with Jay Shetty, featuring Selena Gomez and Benny Blanco stood out because of it's effective use of music. This one podcast has great music in the beginning. But it also have mini flash back recap of the old Selena when she was younger on Disney. This helped bridge her past and present, which then foreshadowed theme of the podcast theme of self-discovery. But the music was soft, ambient background music, while the podcast focus on personal growth, mindfulness, and emotional well being. The placement of the music is also strategic, seamlessly fading in and out at key moments to enhance the natural flow of the discussion. I loved this episode because they explore their love but also how they grew in love and the music in the beginning really set the tone for the rest of the podcast. . The minimalistic nature of the music ensures that it supports rather than distracts from the dialogue, reinforcing the podcast’s calming and insightful atmosphere.
