The podcast I chose is a YouTuber named Kris. I love watching her videos because they are pretty engaging and one way she keeps audiences attention is through the music she choose to use throughout her videos. She uses music like we do, through intros and outros, as well as through dramatic effect. I love when this is incorporated because it entertains me and helps to add a bit more to the podcast. One way she uses music is when she introduces each subject she talks about and gives background. I think this is necessary and important to her particular style because she makes jokes while talking about the main topic of the podcast. When is trying to add comedic effect the music pauses for dramatic effect, and helps to underline said jokes. She also used sound effects to give pictures and words life through swishing sounds etc. One example is when she had a news clipping fly onto the screen, and used the sound effects of paparazzi.
   In my podcast I could see myself following along and also using music to help with certain comedic bits. Like accentuating my emotions and adding certain sound effects to help give the podcast some layers. The use of music could keep users engaged, even if I'm talking about something silly versus serious.
