 I really enjoy listening to comedy podcasts. My 2 favorites have to be 2 bears one cave with Bert Kreischer and Tom Segura and this past weekend with Theo Von. The reason I like the 2 bears one cave podcast is because both of the hosts are comedians and their energy bounces very well off one another. They do a very good job at keeping attention and there is no set-in-stone format, they kind of just riff. I like Theo Von's podcast kind of for the same reason, he is a very funny guy and there is no real format, kind of just jokes and random guests that he has on telling stories. I have no knowledge on how to produce podcasts but I have always wanted to do content creation. I hope this class will get me out of my comfort zone a little bit and teach me how to make this shorter form of content that I can share with the internet. I am less interested in the production side and more interested and excited about making the content and putting it together, it will be cool learning how to edit and hopefully, by the end of the semester, my podcast will have its own character and a sort of theme.
