In the reading I noticed Ali Wong's identity immediately because she straight out tells her ethical background as well as her husband's. She also talks about how she barely knows any Vietnamese, and her mother complains about it because she tries to practice with her mother. I thought that was funny especially since her mom seems like didn't care about her learning or not learning Vietnamese. I chose the WTF podcast by Marc Maron with Cynthia Erivo. In this particular interview it seems like they are just having small talk, and I like this because it makes the people he interviews more comfortable. They talk about Cynthia's roles in movies she's played in and her struggles with playing certain characters. They talk about the culture within these roles going in depth about what these roles meant to her. Cynthia's identity as a black woman living in the UK that was previously religious shows how her identity has shaped her since she was young. Her mother is African, so she is more in tune with that side. She talks about the fact that she used to be a catholic, but it was intimidating and heavy so she no longer practices that particular faith. Cynthia gives insight about how it feels to be a black woman living in the UK. She shows how racism is prominent shaping her world view. While Marc interviews her he uses an informal style especially with how the interview just feels like a big conversation. He also branches off with additional questions after his previous question showing how unstructured this style of interview is.. This in my opinion makes for a great podcast making it personal and feel like the audience is part of the conversation. The talk about raw subjects like slavery and racism show how real the conversation they have throughout the podcast not being weary of certain subjects.
