Link: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/cleaning-with-adhd/id1701600149 I chose a podcast I love to listen to by Darraugh Therese, she is from Ireland and does a cleaning with ADHD. While the music is short and sweet it reminds me of the tone of her voice and feels very lighthearted and easy going. If you know anyone or struggle with ADHD yourself, this podcast is a relatable conversation while you are cleaning or organizing your home. I love how Darraugh starts the podcast with a fun tune and then the rest of it has a melodic tone because of her accent. I think one of the reasons I like listening to her mostly is because her accent is soothing while still being a direct approach. I believe Darraugh chose this music to keep things from feeling daunting. A lot of people don't enjoy cleaning and having someone who enjoys it or can make it easier to get through helps a lot. Sometimes if in person this can be known as body doubling where someone is holding you accountable to your tasks. Darraugh has a lot of great conversation while you are doing things but still jumps right back to the task you are working on to keep you finishing through the end. I think your music choice needs to be in alignment with your topic in some way in order to fit. your niche, I also think it's fun when people change their music even if it's the break to fit the content if their general topics change and it's applicable. I heard a Mom friend say she always plays music for the type of food she serves at dinner for her kids and it's a fun thing to do, I think the same can be done for a podcast when there are different things to celebrate within the topic.
