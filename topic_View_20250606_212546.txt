 A podcast that i have been wanting to check out for a while is called the Smartless, a podcast hosted by Jason Bateman, Will Arnett, and Sean Hayes. I listened to a few episodes this week and really enjoyed it. The podcasts are usually close to an hour, minimum 40 minutes and it is a super cool concept because every show one of the 3 hosts gets to pick a random guest that they will have on the pod and the other 2 hosts don't know who its going to be until they are live. That adds for some cool excitement and we get to feel and hear the reactions of the guys when the surprise guest comes on. There are 3 hosts and 1 guest present in the episodes and they are almost always comedians or actors that they have on the show. It does not really have a narrative, they kind of just riff off one another and talk about whatever they want to in the episodes and listen to stories from each of the new guests. There is not really a specific target audience for this podcast, anyone who enjoys comedy is a fan of any of the 3 main hosts or just likes watching interviews with celebrities would enjoy this podcast. This podcast is kind of how I would like my podcast to be set up after this class, in this class I plan on doing a podcast about movies where I will add some comedy and make jokes but the whole podcast won't be centered around it. These hosts do a very good job at building off of each other and making the guest feel like they are included which is pretty impressive considering the fact that there are at least 4 people in that room with microphones.
