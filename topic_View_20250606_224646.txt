Today, I decided I wanted to step away from the typical platforms I used to listen to Podcasts. Instead of using the iPhone's Podcast app or Spotify, I decided to use RadioLab.
I listened to Haunted. It came out in October of 2014. I filtered all podcasts for ones that came out in 2014 just because I wanted some nostalgia from the year I graduated high school.
 I chose that podcast because of its title. I like all things horror for the most part and really to a certain extent. This podcast was one of the better ones I have ever listened to. To quickly summarize this podcast, it is about a young adult male, Dennis, who has felt like his childhood home has haunted him his entire life. He always felt like it held him back and when he had to return to his childhood home from college after finding out his mother was sick, he felt the house was haunting him once again. Eventually, his mom dies from cancer very soon and so does his dad not long after. His dad was in the middle of helping him find his own home before he passed so now he was stuck with his parents' house. He decided to fix things in the childhood home that his dad had planned on fixing until he realized that those things he fixed would keep becoming unfixed. Then he started seeing his dead parents. After that, Dennis decided to list the house but nobody would buy it because they kept sensing a ghostly presence by the basement door. He got a Ghost-haunter team to examine the house with a flashlight method which turned out to be a load of crap.
There were specific junctures in the narrative that grabbed my attention like Dennis's parents passing back-to-back. That caught my attention because it happened to my dad's parents before I was born. There are musical interludes and music beds that increase the creepy feeling of this narrative. There was very good editing in the construction of this narrative, specifically the inclusion of horror sound effects that grab listeners' attention. This story feels more impromptu than scripted due to parts like when Dennis is hearing the playback from an audio recording years later.
