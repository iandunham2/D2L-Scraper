I chose the episode titled "Death Interrupted" by Radiolab which was released on December 15th, 2023. I specifically chose this one because I saw it and my birthday is December 15th. The episode goes through someone's life as they swapped careers and began working in the ICU. His entire life and career he tended to be saving lives. What caught my attention the most was the word choice and tone used as it just grabs the audience's attention. They use pauses and pacing extremely well. The musical interludes felt very much like sound effects and I didn't necessarily like them because I felt they did not match up at times with what was being said in the podcast. There were times like when talking about the ambulances that sirens played and it did feel to match up but, it also felt too overwhelming. That might of been their intention to make the audience feel how stressful it can be in those types of positions. The editing plays so much into the construction of the narrative as it builds the atmosphere, especially by using these sound effects and musical interludes to build the mood. These sound effects make one's heart drop especially with how they are edited in comparison to the vocals. I will say, the story seems to be scripted just because everything seems almost perfected. No one stumbles over a word and everything flows perfectly as they bounce off of each other going through the story. While editing can allow for continuity and the illusion of bouncing off of each other, usually there are small indications when there is no script to follow. This episode, however, seems very prepared and like they both went in with what they needed to say. Overall, it was a good podcast episode and I learned a lot from it about what I do and don't prefer in podcasts.
