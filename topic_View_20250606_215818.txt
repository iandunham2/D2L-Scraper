A format of podcasting that I seldom listen to is Music Reviews. Although I love music, I seldom listen to music reviews because they often consist of people with biased reviews. For instance, I chose to listen to The Jo Budden Podcast since clips of the show are always popping up on different social media platforms I use. The clips I have seen often revolved around the artists Kendrick Lamar and Drake. Since I'm a fan of both artists, I decided to search for an episode which those clips were coming from. What I did not know is that those clips were coming from multiple episodes which makes sense. It makes sense because Kendrick Lamar and Drake were beefing publicly for most of 2024. Real fans know they have been throwing discrete jabs at each other since 2013 but it escalated in March of 2024 when Kendrick Lamar dropped a lethal diss verse on Future's Like That single. Kendrick Lamar dropped multiple bars in his diss including Drake's recent album name and First Person Shooter track on that same album.
After that diss, Drake decided to fire back with a diss of his own aimed at Kendrick Lamar and others that he believed had a hand in his Push Ups single. Drake then seemingly got impatient with Kendrick Lamar's lengthy vacated time to respond so he decided to drop another track teasing Kendrick. That turned out to be a mistake as Lamar ended up dropping two tracks of his own (Euphoria & Meet the Grahams). Afterwards, Drake responded with Family Matters, which he thought would be his home run until Lamar dropped Not Like Us right after. Not Like Us took the internet by storm and ended up being victorious even though Drake dropped the last diss track in this beef. It wasn't enough.
During the entire flurries of diss tracks being dropped, The Joe Budden Podcast covered the whole thing. It was full of biased opinions because of the host, Joe Budden, having beef with Drake. My reaction was "This is why I do not watch music reviews". People tend to review based on emotion instead of facts. Obviously, everybody is going to have an opinion, but at least include facts mainly. I imagine the production process of this podcast to be chaotic. There is always a lot going on where the show gets out of hand. I do not believe the podcaster prepares for the episode because they play their first reaction live.
