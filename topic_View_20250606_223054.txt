Podcast Link: https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-white-lotus-official-podcast/id1789416962
I think that a podcast that has a good use of music is The White Lotus Official Podcast. The podcast does a deep dive into the newest episode and does a recap of what happened in the episode and the hosts will interview the cast and crew to answer any questions the audience might have. I am a huge fan of The White Lotus so it was really interesting to hear some of the casts thoughts and comments on the episode. This podcast uses music in the beginning and end as intro and outro music. The music sounded like it was the opening credits in the actual show. The song they used was one that sounded like it would be heard playing around in the hotel. It had a nice beat and different drums. I liked how the music also went into the two hosts introductions and not just in the beginning for fifteen seconds. It was in the background but it wasn't to the point where it was to loud and distracting. I think it worked well because I felt more immersed in the episode. They also use it when introducing and talking about the characters for the first time. They also incorporate music when something shocking happens in the show that they are talking about. Overall, the use of music in this podcast really sucks you into the actual show and it makes the audience kind of pause and really listen to what the hosts are saying. This kind of music can help with my podcast since I am talking about different tv shows. Since this podcast used  music from the actual show, I can do that for mine.
