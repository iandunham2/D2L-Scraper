Personally, I feel like sound connects humans with each other, to the world around them, and even themselves. When thinking about sound in my daily life, I truly do not know if it is something I could live without. It is such a beautiful way to connect with the world around you, the people around you, and yourself. For example, being able to communicate with others through talking/listening is such a great experience and it is something I have grown to appreciate more and more. For example, whether you are telling a joke to a friend, sharing a meaningful experience you had, or talking about the day you had, it is all a way to express yourself. Through this self-expression, you are able to discover more about yourself and the world around you. Moreover, sound helps me work through my emotions and feel different ones. A way that I do this is through listening to music. It is such a great feeling being able to work through your emotions and relate to the music you listen to. Whenever I am sad, happy, bored, or excited there is always a song I can play that I can relate to and I truly think it helps me work through that specific emotion.
I feel like I heavily rely on sound in the media I consume as well, it would be an entirely different experience without it. Thinking about this makes me feel grateful for my ability to hear as it is something that goes unnoticed in peoples' day-to-day lives. Even when my airpods die while I am walking to class, I dread the feeling of having to walk without my music or podcast going because it puts me in a good mood. Being able to hear is something I heavily rely on for my mood and emotions throughout the day and without it, I would definitely interpret the world differently.
