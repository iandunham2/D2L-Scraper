   For me I think my sense of sound is everything. I’m always listening to some kind of media, so I think if I were to suddenly go deaf I would think it was the end of the world for me. I do think that if I had never heard recorded audio before I would know that I was missing out on something, but wouldn’t miss it because I had never heard anything before. In reality, if I were to be deaf at birth I would get cochlear implants. I do feel like a sense being taken away is a big loss of discovering the world around us. People do say that those that are limited a sense have heightened senses somewhere else, so I feel like that could be a totally different experience for me.
   Being limited to recorded audio would make me feel like I am missing out on trends and I’d feel like I was set back in time. Although, not being able to see recorded audio could be a good thing because it ideally spreads misinformation and content that isn’t protected, especially against children and vulnerable. On the other hand, recorded media could be a good informant, and keep you up to date on what is happening in the world and social circle. 
   Streaming audio has come a long way since I was a kid. It went from simply streaming music, to YouTube and other social sites sharing streaming audio, to streaming services. The streaming business has grown tremendously. It’s everywhere! I personally stream podcasts or listen to music in the car. I also use streaming services for my information, entertainment, and to socialize. It got to a point where we don’t use cable in my house. We just use Netflix, Hulu, and YouTube TV for all of our streaming needs. I would say that we rely heavily on streaming services, especially audio streaming.
