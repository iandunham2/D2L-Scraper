I think there's a lot of ways that podcasting skills can integrate into a news reporting career. The first way I think of is that podcasting, or at least good podcasting, requires the creator to find interesting topics and a target audience that they want to reach with their topic, and it's the exact same for news reporting. People won't read your stories if it's not something relevant or newsworthy in the same way they won't listen to your podcast if it's a boring or uninteresting topic and not focused.
Also as news becomes more and more digital focused rather than print-focused, learning these skills to produce audio and video content becomes more and more important for career possibilities in the field of journalism. Writing has always been my focus and is still such a huge part of the journalism industry, but it's good to know these other relevant skills that can come in handy because I know employers find it appealing to have employees that have an assortment of skills that can be put to use.
Especially for independent reporting, offering your audience an audio only version of your reporting makes it more accessible and reaches more people than if you have a written only version. Being able to offer more forms of content reaches more people and can grow your independent reporting from reaching a few readers who may only know you personally, to having an audio version that new people can easily try listening to in their car while they're on the way to work or something.
I'm sure there's a lot more possibilities for journalists to use these skills but these are the first ones I think of when applying the skills from this class to my future career. I'm glad I picked up these skills here and can use them going forward.
