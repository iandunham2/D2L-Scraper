Above is a episode of a podcast called dungeons and daddies. It is an actual play podcast played by 4 players who are also have experience in things ranging from tv shows to poetry. Among them is Freddie Wong who is responsible for the sound production that you hear the majority of the podcast. The song that you hear in the end is a commissioned song that plays at the end of every one of these episodes. I like it because it sets the overall tone of this particular season of episodes. The first song though was one that I really enjoyed because it highlights the creativity of the creators. For those unaware this show often likes to open with intros that comprise of two things. First is that they serve as a bit of a recap of what happened in the episode prior. Second they are usually made into a 'bit' that was referenced in the last episode. Not all of their intros are songs but some are as was this one. The beginning song is a parody of One Winged Angel, the theme of Sephiroth in Final Fantasy 7. It references not just the actions of a Nicholas but also references how they attacked a group of men in a similar fashion as sephiroth when infiltrating Shinra HQ
