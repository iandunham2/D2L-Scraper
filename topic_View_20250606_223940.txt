For a podcast in general, I think some noises that could be added are on-sight sounds like people walking past or background noise of a busy area like a the campus green. Or if it was a nature podcast the person could add the sound of leaves rustling on the wind or even sticks or leaves crunching under someone's feet. 
For my podcast, I could include sounds that seem like they would come from the places I am discussing in the Bible. For example, the bustling of marketplaces, footsteps in dusty streets, or the sound of wind through open spaces. This would immerse the listener in the context of the time and place. I could also add sound effects when I mention key concepts and topics like humility or the scripture I am talking about that day. I could also possibly add soft background music that is different from the music I already have when I am reading. That could possibly make it sound a little more interesting and engaging. I could even possibly do an interview of someone and get background sound of what is happening around us in public. I would have to make sure the sound quality is still good though.
