In the reading, I was drawn to the story about Barrack Obama. He talks about what it looked like for him to grow up without a father and how that influenced his sense of identity. He talked about being unsure who to model himself after, and wondering if he needed to be cool or rebellious or manly or tough. He also discussed his college experience and how he realized that as he got older, he stopped trying to act like the people around him and had to discover for himself who he wanted to be as a man. He also discussed absorbing stereotypes of black men unintentionally and what it looked like for him to rewire his brain on those personality traits. I liked the statement he made about recognizing that honesty, kindness, hard work, and responsibility are the most important values no matter how you grow up. I remember those traits the most about him from his presidency. He was a kind and cordial and genuine man, regardless of his political beliefs I thought he did a great job handling the country. I was also very young and know minimal about the economy, but his personality shined through when he spoke!
For the podcast, I picked Ariana Grande. She talked a lot about mental health and how she has navigated being a singer from a young age and being in the spotlight. She is such a kind, genuine person and I could tell by the way she spoke about things. At one point they talked about the grief of loved ones and I realized the things that happen to us also play a HUGE role in who we are and the people that Ariana has lost have affected her identity a great deal. I enjoyed listening to her talk about whys he cares so much about people and things like musicals and it has a lot to do with the way she was raised.
