This module made me realize that sound is something I can’t imagine living without. Being able to hear someone tell me that they love me, hearing someone’s laugh, or hearing a child’s first words are all beautiful things we get to hear and experience in this life. Without sound we would never get to enjoy that beauty. This being said I can’t imagine being deaf and not being able to experience sound in its fullness. I think that it would affect my thought life in that I would always question if I was really experiencing life in its fullness. I would question how present I have been able to be in the most beautiful moments because I can’t fully hear those moments. Furthermore, if I had only heard things in a performance or live context I would be bummed. I have gone to concerts and fallen in love with songs performed live and then started listening to it on repeat afterwards. If this was not an option, I would not be able to relive that moment of me falling in love with that song again. Streaming audio has affected my listening habits because I like to listen to specifics parts of songs to hear specific sounds or lyrics again. With that said, I’m not the most present or patient listener because I either just am anticipating one part of a song or I just skip to that one part of the song. Finally, I think my since of hearing is different from other people in that I interpret “sad sounds” as happy sounds. Sad sounding music (sometimes) makes me feel a sense of nostalgia and comfort. In terms of how this impacts the kind of media that I consume, I consume a lot of emotional media. Because sad and emotional things/ sounds tend to bring me comfort I consume media based on that.
