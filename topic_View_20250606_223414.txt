One great example that I was able to think of in effective music use in a podcast is in Welcome to Night Vale which a fiction podcast that has eerie storytelling with atmospheric music. A specific moment that stands out is the opening theme and how it really shows the show’s tone. The theme by Desperation creates a slow and ominous synth melody with a steady rhythm that immediately makes the listener feel as they are going into the surreal, unsettling world of Night Vale. You can listen to an example here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UcjYD1ZOSiE. 
The effectiveness of this music is in its haunting yet oddly calming quality of perfectly matching the podcast’s mix of horror, mystery, and dry humor. The theme is instantly shown which helps build a strong identity. another thing is the music bed used throughout the show pieces that creates an immersive aural space reinforcing the idea that the listener is stepping into a different world. For my own podcast this example teaches me the importance of choosing music that helps keep the vibe and the mood. Since my podcast explores different illnesses I have and how they affect me and other people and how to create a better life with the illnesses, I try to add a more uplifting music. This way I can create a more positive vibe.
