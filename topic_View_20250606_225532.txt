My podcast have been about tennis news and updates throughout the first part of the year. To wrap up with my final episode I want to take a deeper look into the mental side of tennis and how that effects the minds of all players, professional to junior. Growing up playing tennis, I saw how much the sport relies on mental abilities, and how you can win matches before you even step on a court sometimes. I have yet to talk about that important part of the sport in my podcast, so I want to provide insight into it in the final episode. I've talked a lot about the physical side, just not the mental side of things yet. I also want to provide some context by analyzing different resources that talk about this mental side of tennis. I might also try and get a short interview for this episode, but that is still up in the air a little bit due to scheduling reasons. If I integrate everything I talk about, the production process will take a bit more time just because it will take more research up front and time to come up with questions to conduct the interview.
