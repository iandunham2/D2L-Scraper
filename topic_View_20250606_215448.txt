In Marc Marons reading a clear example of identity that I saw was in his personal exploration of being a comedian and how this interferes with his views on mental health, family, and relationships.  Seems to often reflect on the role his identity as a comedian and his different struggles he has faced with addiction and grief playing into his worldview and how those struggles shaped his perspective on life. This vulnerability tends to create a relatable narrative that allows listeners to see how his identity shapes his worldview and guides the way of his podcast.
I listened to an episode of WTF featuring comedian Tig Notaro. It showed that identity is explored through her personal experience with cancer, her comedy career, and her approach to life as a queer woman. Throughout the interview, Tig openly discusses her experiences with humor as a coping mechanism and how her identity as a queer person has impacted her work while the challenge of navigating lifes toughest moments with a sense of humor. Her identity as someone who has faced significant health struggles and as a queer person shapes her worldview.  This is offering a unique perspective on resilience, humor, and authenticity.
With Marcs interview style being very conversational and empathetic it then is making it a safe space for guests to share personal stories. He asks certain questions that also gives his guests the space to reflect and open up. His approach is thoughtful and is allowing his guests to explore the complexities of their identities and how these different ways are able to shape their perspective on the world. This search of identity in the WTF podcast makes for constant listening because it not only provides insight into the guests personal experiences but also tends to invite listeners to reflect on how identity shapes their own worldview.
