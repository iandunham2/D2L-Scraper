For my podcast, there is not too much that I can think of to do in terms of sound. However, something that I have seen another Christian podcaster do is utilize sound effects. For example, if someone says something that is really profound or encouraging there will be a positive sound effect. Another example is when there is good news that the podcaster is announcing there will be cheering sound effects that play. It is kind of this idea of having a sound board where you can choose sound effects based on the moment that is happening in your podcast. This would be something that I would like to try in my podcast, but I think that it would be more appropriate to do in an episode where I have another guest on it. I think it would be fun to even include a laughing track or an “awkward moment” sound for when someone says a weird joke or something in the episode. I think that this would help my podcast to feel more lighthearted and sillier, as my podcast is a little more serious in nature. Overall, this is for sure something that I would like to try implementing in my sixth podcast episode.
