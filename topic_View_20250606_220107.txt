I decided to listened to a scientific podcast called “Ologies with Allie Ward.” This episode was about Erethizonology or the study of porcupines. The episode was about an hour and ten minutes long and basically, went into anything you could ever want to know about porcupines. The episode centered around an interview with Tim Bean who researches porcupines at Cal Poly. Allie Ward would ask him a bunch of questions she had about porcupines. If Bean didn’t have an answer to one of her questions about porcupines then she would insert a later edit with an answer that she researched. Later in the episode, she took questions from fans and halfway through the episode she brought awareness to the Native American Fish and Wildlife Society.
As for my reaction to the episode, I learned a lot of interesting things about porcupines like how they will eat just about anything even roadkill and bones and that female porcupines are pregnant or lactating eleven months out of the year. However, Allie Ward’s personality grates on me. I wanted her to be quiet, so Tim Bean could talk more. She kept going into dumb tangents like making the talk about porcupine sex even more awkward than it already was and went into trains of thought about porcupines getting high on cannabis since they are attracted to a chemical used in cannabis farming. She just came off as incredibly annoying.
I’m sure this podcast took a ton of research since I don’t really know what else could have been answered about porcupines. It also had an initial filming with the interview and the audience questions and then later had answers being added in post. Allie Ward probably prepared her questions in advance and also put out a notice to her fans about asking questions about porcupines and then had to sort through those questions for once to go on the episode.
