Sound is something that I could not live without. Sound can connect people through music and voice. I know that when I get really stressed, music and film is something that I turn to. There have been so many times where I am blown away by a person's performance on a stage or on the screen that actually makes me feel something. I have connected with other people through music and film and really can't imagine my life without it. Sound really connects people together and brings out emotions that other things can't do.
Having been born with hearing loss, I can definitely relate with how different my life is around hearing. I am not deaf, but I need hearing aids to hear everyday. There have been times where it has affected me. Every time I watch a film or tv show, I have to have the captions on. It helps me hear better when I can actually see the words of what they are saying in front of me. When I am at the movie theatre, I find that it can be challenging. Without the captions I will often find myself missing out on the dialogue and have trouble hearing what they are saying. The same thing goes with live performances. With recorded audio, it gives me a chance to turn up the volume and go back to listen to the words that I missed. Live audio is different as I can't do that. If I had only ever heard audio in a live context I imagine I would be frustrated all the time. The last concert I went to there were multiple times where I had to turn to my sister and ask what the singer said because I didn't hear her and missed what she said. Streaming audio has changed my listening habits because it is readily available for me to consume. I am able to stay up for an album release at midnight and immediately consume it all. I find that since streaming audio is there right in front of me, I will consume it quickly and go through it much faster.
