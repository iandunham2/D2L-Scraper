   During my time recording my podcast I always have ideas in my head of what sound effects could be used at different areas of my podcast. Sound effects and sound isolation would fit well with the nature of my podcast. My podcast topic is conspiracy theories, so naturally the mood for each episode is gonna be pretty eerie and creepy. Therefore, so should the sound effects. The use of sound effects that will set a creepy, haunting mood help to add character to my podcast. Some examples could be like the ones exclaimed in the text, creaky floorboards, the howling wind, or even the use of dead silence. Which brings me to sound isolation.
   Sound isolation is just blocking out unwanted sounds, but could also account for the silence within the storytelling process. The saying "the silence is loud" could be utilized through two different moods: comical or creepy. The use of silence in my podcast is used for comedy or inserting my opinion while telling a story. I used this in my last podcast, but I will try and use it to add a jarring effect along with a comical one. One last thing would be making my own sound effects. This sounds fun, and if I figure out how to utilize this trick effectively then I'd use it in my podcasts.
