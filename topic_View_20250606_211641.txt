Jmitch- Podcasts You Like
Josh Mitchell posted Jan 12, 2025 10:23 PM
Subscribe 
 
 
 
 
 Subscribed
   
 
 One of the many podcasts I like is Cam'Ron and Mase's  "It Is What It Is" podcast.
I like their podcast because it is authentic. One of their mantras is "We say what we want". I feel like that has opened the door for a plethora of podcasts that came after them and influenced them to be themselves. All of the podcasts I viewed before watching It Is What It Is were uptight and professional to the point that it seemed like a strict work environment where they were being micromanaged. There was no personality or fun to it, which made them boring to listen to or watch. On this podcast, there are 3 different personalities. Cam'Ron is the aggressor, Mase is the nonchalant personality, and Stat is the organizer. It is a 3 host format, but sometimes it is a two-host with an interviewee. 
In this class, I'm hoping to learn as much as I can, since I have actually plotted with a couple of my friends about starting a sports podcast. I want to develop the best production skills I can so I can take this skill and use it in my desired career field. Learning about audio will help with way more than just learning how to produce a podcast. This can translate to careers such as Music production, Film production, Car audio installation, etc.
