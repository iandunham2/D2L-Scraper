I will wrap up this season by talking about the NBA Playoffs. I want to talk about what I witnessed going into the playoffs, and also speak on what I have seen in the first week of the playoffs. Then I will end it off by throwing out my predictions of how the playoffs and season will end, and who will be awarded what awards. I am heavily considering integrating a visual of my podcast so I can upload it to YouTube, but I have not decided on doing that yet. That is more so of a bigger commitment due to it adding to my workload. The semester as a whole is coming to an end, so I still have finals in other courses to attend to. Doing that can also affect the flow of the production process. One, now you have to focus on editing the audio AND the video. You also want to make sure the video and audio sync up. That will fall under the editing strategy process of production. The second main thing is music choice and selection. That is very important because of the copyright challenges that will pop up when using YouTube. All of those things will disrupt the flow.
