I think a good use of music in a podcast is music used in Andy Roddick's "Served" Podcast that talks about tennis. The music that is used isn't necessarily "good" music but it is music that holds a purpose. The music used in the introductions is short lived but I think it has the same sort of affect that cheesy music has in commercials. We all have that one commercial that we can remember constantly just based off the cheesy music that plays throughout. I think the intro music to his podcast has this same affect. Even though it's not at all good music and I could never just sit and listen to it, it's simple enough that at this point, whenever I hear that jingle, I know exactly what podcast is about to start. Music like this definitely only fits in with certain types of podcasts and I think sways a lot more towards podcasts with older audiences. For a podcast that has a younger audience, you would definitely want some sort of music that sounds "trendy" in a way to stick to a certain mold and make the listener feel like they are right at home. As far as the music I use in my own podcasts, I just use my own beats and guitar tracks that are piled up on the hard-drive connected to my laptop. I don't think I would change this because it allows my podcast to be a lot more personal and have more of my own touch.
