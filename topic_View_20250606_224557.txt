I chose the episode titled "Uneasy as ABC" by Radiolab that came out on Spotify on August 23, 2024! It was an episode about how a plane crash altered how emergency rooms function! Now, overall the aspect of the narration that caught my attention was how the narrator spoke. The inflection on his voice in every word made it not feel stale. This aspect alone will allow for a listener to stay tuned in for a longer amount of time.  The musical interludes felt more like sound effects, but they were clearly the music breaks. However, they were brief because in this episode they were sandwiched between a commercial. I do not know if I enjoyed it to speak because they had commercials before the episode took off, but you have to pay the bills somehow! The music that plays throughout the background does seem carefully placed and tonally fits with the parts of the story the narrator is talking about. I also found this interesting because the episode seemed half impromptu and half scripted. One side was the narrator telling the story and the other was his friends reacting and asking questions. Sometimes made it hard to follow, but typically the music would jump back in when the narrator resumed the story. The editing does not stand out to me as something phenomenal because I do not think it needs to be. The editing would just have to be the cuts, addition of music, and adjusting the quality of the audio for whoever is speaking. Overall, not my personal cup of tea when it comes to podcasts. Sometimes, I can get bored with the storytelling tone especially with the lack of visuals when listening on Spotify. I did however enjoy dissecting the episode that I chose for this discussion this week.
