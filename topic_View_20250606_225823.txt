As I wrap up this season of the podcast I want the final episode to feel like a full circle moment. Since we have been talking about the generational gap between Gen Z and Millennials, this last episode will reflect on the biggest takeaways, and the most surprising moments, as well as what I personally learned from all the topics of conversation.  When uploading it to Youtube, the production process would most likely need to change a little bit.  I will need to be more intentional about how everything flows not just audio wise but visually as well.
So I will try to probably map out a more structed episode outline, including timestamps and keep the pacing tight to hold attention of many people. I also believe audio quality becomes even more important on Youtube so I can have a clean transition between  both platforms. I want a quiet space and crisp audio while also cleaning things up in post. I most definitely want to keep the editing concise and clean , by making sure I don't have any rough cuts to the point where there are aggressive stop and start points within the podcast. I want the information to come across  as natural but also bringing all of the episode together into one.
