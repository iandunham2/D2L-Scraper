I chose the Mr. Ballen Podcast since it has really good music. The podcast has a “your cool uncle is telling you stories in the 80s and 90s vibe,” so the music is synthetic and retro. It begins while the host, Mr. Ballen, gives the introduction to the podcast, which includes a teaser about the episode. The music is subtle and mysterious and then builds into the theme song for the show. This sequence lasts for about a minute.
Mr. Ballen utilizes music throughout the episode, which is about 40 minutes long. The music is calm while Ballen introduces the murder victim in her ordinary life. Then the music shifts to pensive during the investigation as the investigators work to find out what happened to her. Near the end of murder episodes, Mr. Ballen will give a reconstruction of what police believe happened to the victims and it’s accompanied by creepy music. The moment of the murder is usually punctuated with a raspy wind instrument that sends chills down your spine.
The episode ends with a reprise of the theme song as Mr. Ballen ties up the episode with subsequent information on the story, where to find more episodes, and to thank the listeners.
