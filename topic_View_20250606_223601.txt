I have been actually thinking about this since the beginning of the year. Since I am doing a podcast on serial killers, I thought it would be more enticing to put sound effects when talking about specific moments in order to increase the tension. When talking about moments such as describing how people are being murdered, adding some tension behind what I say with music would likely help portray how scary it truly was. I held off on originally doing this due to me already putting a lot of time into editing and slight concern of losing points for not fully following guidelines. I genuinely think that adding a bit more tension will help people better focus on the podcast as now adays people have very short attention spans, listening to me talk with two music breaks for 15 minutes plus likely isn't the best idea. Having more mentally interactive messages that make them feel tension or curiosity for what I am about to say next seems like a better idea at continuing to keep my audience's attention. I want dramatic effects in order to achieve this and I believe if they are timed well then it will all work out for the better.
