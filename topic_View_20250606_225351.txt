For my last episode of my podcast I planned to wrap up by discussing the impact of working memory disorder talking about its relevance to mental health and everyday challenges. This will tie into the overarching theme of my podcast which touches on how different health conditions can shape our lives and my own. I will discuss the difficulties faced by individuals with working memory disorders and how it affects day-to-day functioning and share personal stories or insights that tie into the more detailed conversation around cognitive health.
Since this episode will also be uploaded to YouTube I think the production process to be a bit more complex than the audio only format. I’ll need to ensure that the flow of the episode is smooth making transitions between topics or segments clear and engaging. For audio quality I plan to maintain the same high standard as previous episodes but I’ll also have to be mindful of visual aspects for YouTube. This might mean adjusting background visuals or incorporating simple graphics or animations to enhance the experience. For music I want to stick with a happy track to maintain a calm and uplifting vibe for this discussion. Editing will be crucial, as I want to keep it concise while still covering the key points without overwhelming the listener. The addition of YouTube means I’ll also need to balance sound levels between the podcast's voice content and any visual elements or audio cues I include, ensuring everything is clear and accessible for both formats.
