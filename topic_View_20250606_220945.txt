I know that there are many theories about the sense of sound that exist out there. I have a theory that the sense of sound affects your quality of life. Whether that effect is good or bad ultimately depends on what sounds you experience. 
Sounds are included in various forms of media nowadays (i.e. music, films, work environment, etc.). There are plenty of arguments surrounding the topic of listening to hip-hop affecting our brains negatively. I disagree with that theory. Why? Because at the end of the day, everything is a choice. Every action has a reaction. There are various subgenres in the hip-hop community. If you are listening to Gucci Mane all day every day, then yes you might be influenced to sell and do drugs. My question is, what about the songs that are not about selling and doing drugs? Nobody tells anybody that they have to listen to negative songs. There are rap songs that exist with positive substance and essence. Gucci Mane has those types of songs like Spotlight, Worst Enemy, and etc.  Then there are artists like J. Cole, Chance The Rapper, and Wale who typically make rap songs in a positive light. The main thing is nobody is forced to listen to negative music and if so, the artists of those songs are not telling you to copy them.
The sounds you hear and experience at work could also affect your quality of life. For instance, I work in a mechanic shop at a car dealership. I have been doing this for 4 years now. When I first started, I would hear random noises once I got home after a long work day. Once I got to some peace and quietness in my room, I would sporadically hear hammers banging, oil pumping, and new tires scrubbing against epoxy flooring. While I do not experience those episodes now, I still have not gotten used to the noise of new tires scrubbing. I felt miserable my first 2 years of working here because of that. Now that I have adjusted, it is not so bad.
