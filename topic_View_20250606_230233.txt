   Hello, so I never really thought of doing podcasts as a career. I feel like it would definitely be something I do for fun with friends, and/or something I post online for extra cash. I do see myself using some of the tools however to go into the career I wanted to do. I would like to be in production as a videographer, that is my end goal. I feel like I could use some of the skills we learned to combine the two and make a pretty cool podcast.
   If I am really going to be honest, I would only see myself doing this professionally if I was just a part of the recording process. That is my favorite part, talking, just being able to feel like I'm having a conversation with my viewers like in Kallmekris' podcast or in Rotten Mango. I love that part about it, making it personable and I do love to talk especially when its about something I find intriguing. If I could just film the video and send it to an editor like YouTubers do I would love to do it. I do find the whole editing process very tedious, especially when I don't have time to sit and do it because of other priorities. Maybe it would be more enjoyable if it was less of a hassle, I'm not too sure.
   The way we use the software and are able to edit with the software I feel would be very useful in my career as a videographer as we'll have to edit footage. Taking the use of dynamics and EQ from this class could really help with maximizing the sound quality of my videos, so I am happy to have that skill under my belt when I get into my future career!
