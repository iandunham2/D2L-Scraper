For the next podcast, I would like to add sound effects that will help listeners visualize where I am recording. For example, I may do a few sound bits in the beginning to introduce myself. I might do the sound of a door opening and closing with footsteps approaching the mic. It will sound like I am entering the room and closing the door. The sound of the door being shut indicates that the only people who know what I am saying in the room are the listeners. In a way, it will provide a sense of connection between me and the audience. I want my podcast to be a little less serious, and a little more natural. Sound has a way of letting listeners understand where the podcast is about to go. I might try to add some other effects such as the no signal tv sound effect. That could help me transition from the introduction into the actual content within my podcast. My goal in added extra sound effects is to not over do it. I don't want it to sound like a bunch of random sound bits. Some content creators will do that, but I feel like it would not work well for 15 minute podcast episodes.
