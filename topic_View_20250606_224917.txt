One episode of the GET REAL podcast that really stood out to me was Season 4, Episode 19: “Harsh Reality of Beauty Standards,” featuring Ashley, BM, JUNNY, and Peniel. It came out back in September 2024, but it still feels incredibly relevant. The group dives into a raw and honest conversation about how beauty standards, especially in Korea, have impacted them personally and professionally. A moment that really grabbed me was when BM talked about the pressure to look a certain way as an idol. He mentioned how he used to feel insecure about his appearance, even when fans constantly complimented him. JUNNY and Ashley also chimed in with their own stories, including how beauty standards differ in the West and Korea, and how those expectations followed them across borders.
The episode doesn’t use much music, which honestly works in its favor. The lack of heavy production makes the conversation feel more intimate and natural, like you’re just hanging out with friends who happen to be opening up about something deep. Any background music that does appear is super subtle, almost like a light touch to help ease transitions without disrupting the mood. Editing-wise, the flow is smooth. The conversation feels organic, but you can tell the editors trimmed things just enough to keep it on track. It doesn’t feel overproduced or forced, and that balance is key for this kind of storytelling.
