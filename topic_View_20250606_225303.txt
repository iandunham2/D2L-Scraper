For the last episode of my podcast season, I am going to be reviewing the movie "Sinners" featuring Michael B. Jordan and Hailee Steinfeld. I just saw this movie tonight and it was phenomenal, I think it will be the perfect way to wrap up this class! As for taking this podcast to YouTube as well, I think that could be very fun/exciting. It would definitely make the editing process different but, it would be a challenge that would help me grow my editing skills. I have always been interested in posting these reviews to YouTube so, this would be a perfect opportunity. I would have to make sure I have a good visual set-up which includes things like background, lighting, and camera quality. It would be more than just audio, it would be all of the visual aspects that come along with production as well. With posting it on YouTube, I think it may have a better chance at reaching more people especially if I begin to post clips of it on YouTube shorts or TikTok to promote my channel. Overall, I think it would probably help my talking flow and editing because for me, it feels easier to edit visuals and audio rather than one by itself.
