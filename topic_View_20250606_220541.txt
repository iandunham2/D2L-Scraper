My historical knowledge about the introduction of sound into the daily human life would need a refresher. However, I want to preface this discussion post by providing a stance I am very adamant about. I have seen a "would you rather" question that offers the choice of no sound, music, etc but tv and video then vice versa. I have always been on the side that I would keep music and sound even though I love tv, videos, and movies. I would say people would say music first when it comes to their thoughts about sound. Now, why do I feel this way. I would decree music and sound as something universal like math. It does not matter the language of the song as long as one can discern the melody. It does not matter where one is at, they will be able to discern what a siren or thunder means. I believe music is one of the best ways to introduce different cultures just like food. This discussion has led me to view sound almost like a culmination of ingredients whether it is pitch, frequency, bass, or treble.
I really find the idea of if I was deaf interesting. Sometimes, it can appear that way to my parents that I am; anyways, if I just became deaf I would have such a hard time adjusting. I spend the majority of my time listening to music when participating in some of my hobbies. I believe my world would become more bland without sound. I enjoy aspects like hearing a lively restaurant when celebrating a birthday. If I born fully deaf I would have to navigate life in ways that allow me to "hear" sound in whatever way I can like Beethoven feeling vibrations to construct his music. At that point, sound becomes attached to another sense allowing me to "experience" it.
