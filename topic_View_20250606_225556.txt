I'm going to wrap up my podcast's season by continuing from the end of the last episode to make a sort of two-part episode, though people can listen to both independently and still understand it. The first "part" was talking about Drew Brees football career before he made it to the Saints and how he got there, and this part will be about his career with the Saints, from beginning to end and the aftermath. It should be pretty straightforward as far as the flow, going chronologically through his years as the quarterback for the Saints. The music I'll use will be a mix of some inspirational, softer music and definitely some more triumphant music for when I'll be talking about the Saints' Super Bowl victory. I'll use a lot of crowd cheering sound effects, and lots of broadcast snippets of highlights, his retirement announcement, things like that to help create a sense of how big some of the events were for fans of the Saints and also football fans in general, because it did have an impact on all of the NFL. It will be a fitting end to the season for the podcast recounting the events of the career of the most impactful player the Saints have ever had.
