In researching my podcast episode about newborns and nutrition, I will start with looking in the Kennesaw State University Library system for my Peer Reviewed Academic Source. I was glad to find a plethora of information in the database on this subject specifically on breastfeeding. The second place I researched for information were in journalistic type magazines. However I found it a little harder to find what I was looking for since I don't pay for access to some of them, I was able to find a wonderful article however on parents.com where just a few days ago they posted a study on Rural parents who give up on breastfeeding sooner. For the third source I am going to research health mentor Barbara O'Neill who I just saw in a health conference speak on child nutrition. I found one of her clips on YouTube and was able to apply that towards my research for this episode. I will integrate these articles throughout my dialogue when it comes to the length of time and discussion on solid foods and the introduction of such into their diet. I am thrilled to discuss this topic as it is under discussed with a lack of education within our communities leaving mothers to find and dig for it themselves. Our bodies are incredible with the way they are able to assist a new life with nutrition and it's important to educate mothers on these topics with stories of success and failure so they might learn from others shortcomings on their journey.  I am excited to share the knowledge I have gained with others to help mothers during this crucial time.
