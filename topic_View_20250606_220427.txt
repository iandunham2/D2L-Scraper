Sense of sound is obviously crucial to so many everyday things we do, but it's hearing people's voices that I would miss the most if I were to go deaf. One of the first things you think of when you're thinking of someone you love is the sound of their voice and the things they would say, so it would be devastating for me to lose that. I'm always trying to find new bands and artists to listen to as well, so not being able to hear any music would be another huge loss for me.
Not hearing any recorded audio would be an interesting experience, a lot of learning would be missed out on if it wasn't possible to record and playback audio. So many educational sources are audio or video that it would likely limit a lot of the things that people know, not to mention that most people probably wouldn't know too many songs because they would have had to hear them performed live. Having to read every single thing to be able to learn it, except I guess for when listening to a lecture or something, would get very boring after a while.
Streaming audio has affected my listening habits by giving me access to so many artists and podcasts from genres and topics that I might have never been able to listen to otherwise. Without the ability to do so, I could have been stuck just exploring a couple of genres from artists in my area or something like that. While maybe I could have found something great, I think I've been able to find a lot of good stuff on my own through streaming access.
