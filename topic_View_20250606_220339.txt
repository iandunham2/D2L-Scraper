Truthfully, I don't believe I would be able to properly function if I were to just currently go deaf. The only way I am able to do assignments or focus on a single task is if I got multiple forms of stimulation in the room such as currently I have a movie on my second monitor and music playing. I have been told many different times that this is extremely weird but, it is the only way I am able to properly focus on getting stuff done, and half the time I am singing along to the song and taking mental notes on the movie for other classes. If I were born deaf, I believe I would have adapted over time. 

If I had never heard recorded audio before, I think I'd be longing for it, I am not a huge concert person so going and seeing things performed live would be very stressful for me. I love music and films so I likely would be wishing for something that allowed for audio to be recorded and replayed. I'd possibly be so motivated that I would figure out a way to do it myself since that is something I tend to do. Streaming audio has highly affected my listening habits just because it is constantly playing in the background. I listen to music so much that my roommates use it to determine if I am home or not. No music is being heard from outside my door and I am not home. I had a headache the other day and ended up not having any sound for the entire day and when I went downstairs, my roommate got scared at seeing me thinking I was an intruder for a second. Audio is a huge part of my life and tends to help me keep my head on straight so I entirely need it. I am overly dependent on noise or having multiple things to interact with and I always tend to keep a stimulating environment around me.
