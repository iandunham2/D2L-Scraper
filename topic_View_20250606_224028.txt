The next episode in my podcast is talking about Glee and the lgbtq+ relationships in the show. I mainly just talk about the show and explain if the show has good or bad representation and what it means to me and other people. I think with this episode, I can incorporate sound design when I start talking about different scenes. Glee is a show about high school students where they are mostly in the choir room singing songs. When talking about a scene that takes place in there or the school or hallway, I can add sound of peoples footsteps in the background or if I talk about a characters battle with religion and sexuality, I can add religious music in the background or the sound of a choir. Glee also has a distinct song that plays in the background of the show and I can definitely add that in the beginning. One thing that Glee says in the beginning of all their episodes is that they do a recap and then they say "and that's what you missed on Glee" so I can definitely say that when I am doing a recap of the show and introducing the characters when I open my podcast.
