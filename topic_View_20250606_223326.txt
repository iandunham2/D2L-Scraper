Link: What Do Moms Think About Dating? I CCB Podcast Season 9 Episode 6 
  For this week’s module I chose a podcast by Candace Cameron Bure. Every season of her podcast has a different vibe, as her guests change every season, but the heart of her podcast is the same. Candace brings this positive, upbeat, and uplifting vibe to her podcast and I feel that her uses of music fits that vibe well. She does not do anything out of the ordinary with her music choice. Mainly her music use is just in the preview of the episode at the beginning and in her intro. The music in the preview is very anticipatory, if I had to use a word to describe it. In this preview Candace plays a very interesting part of the podcast to come and then cuts it off right before anything is said/ happens to reel in her audience. I think that this is effective because the music sets a tone that makes you want to lean in and makes you anticipate the conversation to come. Even though Candace has a preview she still has a formal intro as well. Candace kind of explains the heart of this podcast and how it's about us doing life together and then an image of her pops on the screen and fades. There is farm-like/ barn music that plays while this image is on the screen. I think that this is effective because it gives the vibe of a family friendly kind of setting, which is exactly what her podcast is. Candace does not have an intro, but I actually think that it's strategic with having all the musical elements of her podcast at the beginning. I like how it's organized where you know that all of the music parts happen at the beginning, and you have the rest of the podcast to listen completely to the topic. I think this is all helpful for me to choose music based on the environment I want my podcast to be. I think that I currently just have upbeat music for my podcast, but I really want to have music that does give a young adult kind of tone.
