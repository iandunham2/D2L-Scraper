For my podcast episode, I will try and find something on the tv show A League of Their Own. I am focusing on the gender, race, and sexuality part of it so I don't think it will be that hard to find some sources. I plan on using the KSU library to find an academic journal article. There are a lot of videos on YouTube where the actors talk about the show and what it means to them and fans. I know I will find something on there to further understand my topic on why the show is so important to people. There are some articles online that talk about the history of the show as well as the history behind the baseball team which I think will help give some background on my topic. I think there are some articles out there that talk about the themes on the show, specifically the LGBTQ+ relationships and what they went through. Since the show is loosely based on the movie and the movie is based on the real team, I am sure I will find some articles explaining what these women went through and their thoughts about the show.
