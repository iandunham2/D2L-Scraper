I found a podcast episode from the Radiolab podcast. The episode was titled Zeroworld and aired December 29, 2023. I was initially interested in the episode because of topic. Karim Ani was brought on to discuss something groundbreaking that he discovered in regards to the biggest rule in math. The rule is to never divide zero and Karim explains what actually happens when you divide by zero. He found that a person is left with infinity if they divide anything by zero, and it proves that all numbers equal the same thing. Steve Strogatz was brought on to add more context in regards to mathematics. 
I really enjoyed the content within the episode, but I personally was not a big fan of the editing. It sounded like a really edited version of an interview. It also felt random whenever the host would chime in and speak. If you listen to the episode, you might understand what I mean. In terms of what I did like about the editing, there were a few aspects that I felt hit the mark. First off, the editing did speed up the episode. I assume than most people do not want to listen to a 2 hour long podcast about the number zero. The quick jumps from person to person made the explanation sound a lot more simple and easy to understand within 30 minutes. The music interludes were a great addition and helped set the tone. The narrative did sound scripted based on how the host would speak. However, for this format it worked really well. The podcast takes a topic that may sound boring or confusing, but then they explain it in a story-like way. It's like a podcast version of the Netflix show Explained but without the visuals. There are many people who commented on the episode and enjoy Radiolab, so what they are doing is definitely working.
