Link: https://radiolab.org/podcast/mixtapes-to-the-moon
The podcast that I found was from Radiolab called "Mixtapes to the Moon". This episode aired on May 24, 2024. I really liked this episode as it talked about the Walkman and how it came to be as well as why music is important. It then transitioned into talking about the three crew members on Apollo 11 and how they each had a Walkman and how that kind of helped them in space and why it was so important. One of the things that caught my attention was the different media artifacts the host used. He used a lot of clips from the Nasa control room talking to the astronauts as well as clips from the astronauts talking. He also used different sound effects like when he talked about someone picking up a Walkman, he used the sound effect of someone hitting play and then added music. The podcast used a couple different music interludes to transition into different topics and they used a kind of spacey sci-fi music to keep the theme of space. I thought it was really well done and the music breaks really kept you sucked in with the feel of space. The editing plays a huge role as this episode had a lot of suspenseful moments. When the episode talked about the third person on Apollo 11 circling alone around the moon in the dark really highlighted how alone he was and how scary it was. There was no music and mainly just static similar to what the astronaut would hear on the ship. It was definitely one of the most intense parts of the episode and the editing made it so that the listener would feel that. The story felt like it was scripted since there had to have been a lot of research going into it. The host knew what to talk about next and definitely had a clear view of what each segment was going to cover. Overall, I thought this episode was very well put together and had me hanging on to every word near the end because of how suspenseful and thrilling it was.
