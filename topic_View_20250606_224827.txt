Link: The Times They Are a-Changin' | Radiolab Podcast 
For this week’s discussion I chose to listen to a podcast by Radio Lab. I listened to the episode titled “The Times They Are a-Changin'." In this episode they tell the story about a discovery they learned about that made them question just how big a year is/ the flexibility of time. Specifically in this episode, the things that got my attention the most were the sound effects used, the humorous remarks/ moments, and their overall use of music. All of these elements kept me awake and alert as I listened to this episode, and I was able to focus better on what was being talked about. I think this is cool because they also are open to children and adults watching this podcast and you can tell how this podcast would appeal to a younger audience. Also, in this episode they used musical interludes to set the mood/ the tone of what was happening in the narrative. For example, music was used in a moment to portray a silly tone/ mood while they listed off theories/ thoughts that people had relating to this topic. Furthermore, the role that editing played in the construction of this podcast is helping us to attain more facts about this topic of how flexible time is. We not only were listening to what the hosts were saying but clips were included of other conversations that some of the hosts had with other people about this topic. They constructed the episode in such a way that we were just able to hear from multiple outside sources. The story is told in a scripted/ organized manner. I can tell that they put some thought into how they were going to present this discovery and how they use corals to help them present it as well. Also, they had their facts, studies, and the outside audios supporting what they said timed appropriately throughout the episode as well. Overall, this was a podcast episode that had an effective audio narrative!
