I think the prospect of me being deaf can change depending on whether I was born deaf or if something later in my life influenced me in a way that I became deaf like an accident. For the first option, I think that being born deaf would be difficult for me for a variety of reasons. We use sound for a lot of things from entertainment to learning. Theres also mediums like music and podcasting that depend solely on the person's ability to hear. But I also feel like it wouldnt be nearly as hard as someone who was gone deaf and lost there sense of hearing when they clearly had one before. Like I feel like in some ways its like if you were to ask me what its like to not be able to see in infared like some animals can. I wouldn't really know what Im missing, On the other hand someone who had lost there hearing would have the struggle of having actually been able to hear things and not being able to do so anymore which I think would lead to a terrible way of living. although that's not to say of course that the other way of living, being born deaf is much better. Perhaps if our lives as humans didn't place such emphasis on hearing it wouldnt be a huge deal but that is clearly not the case. As today the audio medium is so big that we are literally taking a college level course about it. It is worth noting though that I think that this deaf situation is not quite as bad as it would be with sight, I think there are also some other negative impacts in that a lack of hearing would severely limit the people you interact with. Not just because you may struggle to communicate but also you cant interact over communities built around stuff based on audio mediums like hit songs or podcasts

Hi Reggie,
You bring up a really interesting point about the difference between being born deaf versus losing your hearing later in life. I agree that if someone was born deaf, they wouldn’t really know what they were missing, kind of like how we don’t think about not seeing in infrared. But for someone who loses their hearing, that adjustment would be so much harder. Going from experiencing music, conversations, and all the little sounds of life to complete silence would be a huge change. I also think it’s true that our world puts a massive emphasis on sound, especially with how big audio-based entertainment has become. Music, podcasts, and even just the way people connect through shared sounds or viral audio clips would be hard to experience without hearing.

One of the big limitations for hard of hearing and deaf people recently was during COVID-19 and people were wearing masks and deaf and HOH people couldn't read lips. I know some people in that community and that was incredibly hard for them. Our two biggest senses I think around the world is hearing and seeing and not having one of those has its challenges, but I do think at least in the Western world that subtitles on films and shows are much easier to find for example. When I was growing up, my family and I had a close relationship with my piano teacher and we had to make sure movies had subtitles before we put them on to watch to make sure we included her.
