I always have the debate with my friends about what would be harder, going deaf or going blind. My answer is blind 100% and I believe most people would agree with me. I know that’s not the point of the reading but that’s the first thing I thought of when opening this module. If I were to go deaf I think I would lose so many important aspects of my life. I love listening to music, watching tv and movies, talking to friends, pretty much everything I do besides sports requires me to have the ability to hear. Sound is very important in my every day life and this reading made me realize that. 

I love going to concerts and seeing movies on big theatre screens so I think my perception of things would be very different if I never heard recorded audio before. There is alot of music that has auto tune on it and isn’t real so I think music would be more authentic to me if I only heard it live and up close. I would loose alot of my hobbies as well because I wouldn’t be able to listen to music or watch movies. Streaming audio has greatly affected my listening habits. I always think about my parents and their parents and how each generation it became easier to listen to music and television. My parents had records and tapes and they couldn’t just pull out a phone and start playing a song where ever or whenever they wanted to. I think I would still really enjoy listening to music but I don’t think I would do it as much if I had to listen to music by going to the store and buying a record and putting it on and listening to the whole thing. The ease of technology helps a lot these days.
