 The podcasts that I find myself listening to the most are actual play DND podcasts. Examples of such podcasts include The Adventure Zone, Dungeon and Daddies, NADPOD, and Dimension 20. Its also worth noting that in addition to the fantasy and action narrative that these podcasts often take the form of, I also find myself a fan of the humor interweaved into it. I understand that some other podcasts about dnd and other TTRPGs arent as humor-centered as the ones I listed but I realized that my personal preference leans toward the sometimes goofy nature.
Aside from greeting the credits that I need. I think the one thing I want to get about of this class the most is a better understanding of the podcasting space. The hosts of the adventure zone, the Mcelroy's don't just host this podcasts but also have their own podcasts that are successful enough for them to make a career our of it. I am interested in exactly what it takes to make this kind of content and how it may differ from other forms of media such as videos on youtube and maybe even consider a career in it
