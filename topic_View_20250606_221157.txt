For this episode, the KSU EBSCO database is an easy to access source for academic papers that I can pull from to find something relevant. I've used it many times to find sources for other assignments and papers so using it shouldn't be an issue. Another place I find a lot information is Wikipedia; but not directly from the Wikipedia page itself. Pretty much every Wikipedia page has its sources listed at the bottom of the page, and are often reputable and reliable sources. It's a great way to dig deeper into a subject by going through some of these links and seeing what you can find. YouTube of course is the other place I'll look, and it should be easy enough to find discussions or video essays about the Saints football team and their history. Pretty much every sports franchise has a dedicated fanbase, no matter how big or small, that makes in-depth content about the team and its history so I'm confident I'll be able to find some good things. The gist of how I'll incorporate them into my episode is that I'll bring them up, mostly discussing how people viewed the "Bountygate" saga of the Saints franchise at the time, and what they took away from it. It's got a historical element to it so that's what I want to focus on with the sources.
