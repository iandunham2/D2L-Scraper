For this week's discussion, I chose the podcast "Crime Junkie". In each episode, they talk about a different true-crime case and go over all of the case details. Not only do they do a good job at breaking down all of the details, some of their episodes have actually helped solve cases that have been desolate for years. As for the music in their episodes, when they have ad breaks it is always very intentionally placed at a point where the listener is left feeling curious/eager as to what they will say next. The music also has a very mysterious vibe behind it and it makes a difference being that it is placed strategically throughout each episode. If it was randomly placed in each episode, it would feel super out of place and confusing to listeners. You can tell the editor takes the time to find a spot where an ad break/music would feel the best and flow the best with the rest of the podcast episode. I linked an example below to one of their recent episodes and here, you will be able to find their intro music in the beginning and the various music breaks in the middle of it for ads. 
https://youtu.be/EHTRWyPFWug?si=6skeO-JeX7r32Ua_
