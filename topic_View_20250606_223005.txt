This week when I was thinking about podcast music and how it affects the tone and pacing of a podcast I decided to go and listen to an old reliable. This past weekend with Theo Von is the podcast I chose to talk about because he has his own unique intro and outro music. I think this is super cool when podcasts do this because then it brings a sense of singularity to the podcast and sets it apart from others. It's hard for us to do that in this class because we don't really have a production budget and I feel as though that is needed for songs. Theo Von's song is catchy and it is not too long, it just lets you know what podcast you are listening to in a fun and goofy way. Theo Von's podcast is very goofy and it's typically a lighthearted podcast so the music matches that theme and keeps viewers relaxed and lets them know something funny is coming. Adding music at the beginning and end is typically what I see most podcasters do so that the episode isn't interrupted by ads and music, which would just be annoying for the listeners if they had so many interruptions.
