For the fourth episode of my podcast, I will be discussing one of the ultimate pieces of queer media: "Ru Paul's Drag Race". This show not only is important in the LGBT+ sphere, but many people outside of our community have come to know and love the competition show where drag queens face off for the ultimate supreme title. For my research, there will lots of digging and sifting online to find what I need as far as videos, interviews with hosts and competitors, and articles about the show. Ru Paul Charles' show has amassed 17 seasons from the original show, 10 seasons from the "All Stars" spin off, and over 30 international seasons from countries like Mexico, Canada, and the UK. I'd like to include in my episodes quotes from interviews with former or current cast members, as I think it will help verify the queer experience of being a watcher to a competitor on "Drag Race". I also want to include articles about the show and Ru Paul, as I think her influence on drag culture since the late 80's has been the most influential especially when bridging the gap between queer and straight cultures.
