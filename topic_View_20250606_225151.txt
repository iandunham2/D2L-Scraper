For Episode 7, I’m planning to wrap up the season with a more reflective tone. I want it to feel like I’m sitting down and talking directly to the listeners, just kind of looking back on everything we’ve covered and how the podcast has grown. I might include a few callbacks or moments from earlier episodes to tie things together, but overall, I want it to feel natural and not overly edited.
I decided not to upload this episode to YouTube. I’d rather keep the focus on the audio experience and make sure it sounds as polished and engaging as possible. Adding visuals would shift my attention away from that, and right now, I just want to focus on telling the story well through sound alone.
For music, I’m thinking something more calm and nostalgic to match the “season wrap-up” vibe. I’ll also pay extra attention to the flow and pacing since this episode is more about reflection than introducing new ideas. My goal is to end the season in a way that feels complete but still leaves room for curiosity about what might come next. I feel as though that is the best approach to end my series in a satisfying way.
