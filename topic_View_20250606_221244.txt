For the fourth episode of my podcast, I utilized the KSU library database to find an academic journal related to my episode topic. I also browsed the internet for reliable publication articles regarding my topic, I ended up going with one from TIME Magazine. As for the video source, I was able to find an interview that was done with someone my topic pertained to and it turned out to provide a lot of valuable information coming from someone who experienced this situation first-hand. It was honestly quite fun to search for different sources regarding my topic and it allowed me to gain greater knowledge about the topic my episode was on. Typically, I would of course do some research before each episode but, having to look for three sources from different kinds of publications allowed me to gain broader information. I will definitely do this more in episodes after this, I also think it gives me more to discuss in each episode which is always beneficial. Moreover, I would like to think the audience member would enjoy the podcaster pulling up what other people have researched/said about that certain topic because it can give them more of a perspective on it. Overall, I enjoyed adding these sources into my podcast and it is something I would like to keep doing!
