I will be honest. I am not big into podcasts, and it is tough for me to listen to ones that have no visual presence like a video, etc. I find myself viewing some of the sports podcast clips occasionally on TikTok or Instagram. I do not know what is in me where I do not like listening to podcasts, but I have made a couple for a previous class and I love it. The podcast I attached is a sports podcast that I watch, and the host plus the visuals they include are able to keep me interested. The host has a great way of including topics and hypotheticals that make it feel like a conversation.
For this class, since I have made a couple podcasts in Adobe for another class I chose to take this class in hopes of learning how to improve my podcast skills! I hope that I can look at all my projects holistically and see improvement in each one. The podcast workflow is something I am interested in learning! I have a good understanding on how to get great audio, but I think I need help on how to expand ideas so I can perpetuate the length of each.
