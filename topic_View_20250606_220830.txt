Sound is deeply intertwined with our emotions, experiences, and the way we see the world around us. As I reflect on this it becomes much clearer on how much sound shapes my environment and how different it would be without it. For me sound is more than just something that happens in the background but it actively informs and enhances my understanding of many different situations, emotions, and people. Whether it's a conversation, music, or environmental noise sound helps to ground me in my reality which is providing what I’m experiencing.
If I were deaf I imagine my thought on life might be different. Without the ability to hear I would likely rely more on vision and reading body language, watching facial expressions, and observing my surroundings in ways that a hearing person might not. My thoughts throughout the day might also shift. I could see myself thinking in terms of movement, color, and emotion rather than in words or sounds. With this idea I think I would likely be more abstract and relying on imagery rather than sounds to create my problem solving. The absence of sound would not necessarily eliminate my ability to communicate, but it would certainly alter the means and depth of my communication. I would likely lean more on written language, sign language, or other forms of expression that convey meaning in ways not reliant on sound.
When imagining what it would be like to never have heard recorded audio and only live sounds, I was wonder how different the concept of "time" and "memory" would be. Recorded audio whether it’s music, podcasts, or even conversations has been allowing us to capture moments in time and revisit them whenever we like. Without recorded audio I definitely would be limited to living in the present moment. I think memories would be more lost, as we rely so heavily on recorded audio to preserve moments. This is whether it's a song that reminds us of a specific time in our lives or a conversation that we want to replay.
Streaming audio has drastically changed how I and many people engage with sound. Unlike traditional radio or CDs, streaming allows for on-demand access to any song or podcast at any time. This means I engage with music in a different way like playlists for specific moods, moments, or activities. I’m also more likely to listen passively and letting music or different audio to fill the background while I do other things. To sum up, sound shapes our perception in ways that we often take for granted. Without sound or with limited access to it thing like our thoughts, environments, and habits would be vastly different. Sound connects us to our memories, our emotions, and our identities, and it shapes the way we interact with the world around us.
