One person whose identity was visible through the narrative was Barack Obama. Obama tells of how he wrestled for many years with who was supposed to be as a black man. He did not have his father in his life, so he really had to learn how to be a black man specifically through trial and error. Obama explains how he assumed other identities out of insecurity and immaturity (ex: being rebellious, tough, or cool). I love how at the end of his narrative he explains how he is a mixture of the values his mother instilled in him and who he has found himself to be as a black man (apart from the world’s stereotypes).  
The episode I listened to was the one with Ariana Grande. In summary, this podcast episode they talked about Ariana’s upbringing when it came to her singing career, how important the song “Over the Rainbow” is in her personal life (with her grandpa) and her career today (with Wicked), Ariana gave many details about her parents lives and how they raised her, and they also just talk about many other different topics within the industry (for example how ambition is about perspective). Marcs interview style is very conversational. It feels like he is just listening to her and asking good follow up questions that kept the conversation flowing. I really enjoyed this natural and laid-back approach. I think that this podcast involved the exploration of identity through talking about Ariana’s parental upbringing. She mentioned that her mom was not one of those “crazy stage moms” and that her mom was a career boss woman. As well as her dad had his own thing going for himself. With these two parental aspects combined it left Ariana with the freedom to choose her own path without worrying that her parents were trying to mooch from her/ without pressure. You can tell that this freedom has shaped who she is as a person and specifically how her mom being such a career "boss woman” has shaped how hard she works today. Overall, I think this makes for a compelling podcast episode because we get to see what Ariana Grande was taught from an early age. We get more insight into how her parents taught her to think and how she has truly taken those things with her into her now successful career. It is compelling to see how someone as successful as her was brought up and taught.
