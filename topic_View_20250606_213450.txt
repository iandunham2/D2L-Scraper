 My podcast is about A24 films. For each episode I am planning on diving into one of the A24 films that interests me, I summarize that movie and then I talk about what I thought about it. A lot of A24 films are super weird and tend to have a deeper meaning behind the films so I am going to say what I thought the films meant and I am also going to have guests on who have different perspectives and take on things. I will talk about the reviews the movie has gotten and whether or not I agree with some of these reviews and I will also talk about the way that other people on the internet have interpreted these films. I decided to pursue this topic because I really love watching movies and I think it's something that I can talk about with passion and I feel like what I have to say might interest people who care about movies as well. A24 films are very hot right now and everyone has seen at least a few of them and talk about them with their friends. Listening to anyone talk about anything they are super passionate about is interesting usually because the person cares so much about that topic that it can excite listeners. The biggest challenge that I can think of is that if this wasn't a podcast for a podcast class I would have limited material to talk about because there are only so many A24 films and they only drop a few a year so I would have limited content or I would have to change production companies once I was done with these films. There is a Podcast called the A24 podcast and it has the actors from A24 films on the show and talks about what it was like being in those movies and their interpretations.
