 This was the first ever podcast where I had someone else join me. My topic is more niche, so I had to find someone who was capable of discussing my topic intelligently. I think this provides compelling content for my podcast because it is all about dropping knowledge of sports. My interviewee was someone who loves the sport I talk about just as much as I do. I believe the passion is present when listening. This episode was longer than my first which I do believe interview episodes need to be longer to allow for a conversation. I like more of the inquisitive personality for the interviewer. I was curious on his takes. I meandered through thoughts and topics to allow for questions to arrive once they hit my mind. I think the most important moment for this interview was making sure we provided intelligent thoughts because once again my podcast is built on being knowledgeable about college basketball. I wanted to talk about something that allows for different thoughts and bracketology and final four predictions were the best topic for this episode when interviewing a fellow basketball lover. I think I excelled more in this interview than the last interview.
