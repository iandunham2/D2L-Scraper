For this episode, I will use the library to search academic sources about my topic, which is the Gospels. I think I will have a slightly difficult time finding academic sources that include scripture, but I am sure I will be able to find something. I will also search on The Gospel Coalition for my journalistic source. This website contains many commentaries on the bible by reputable pastors and sources. I can also use Enduring Word, which is another Bible commentary. I will be able to research the Scripture and stories that I have picked for this week, get extra historical and biblical context, and understand more details about the meaning. For my video sources, I will either use the show The Chosen or a YouTube video of a sermon from Auburn Community Church where Miles Fidell is the pastor. These sources will be effective in having visual representations of the character of Jesus and the way that he lived on Earth and cared for his people. It is sometimes easier for us to understand things in a context that we are used to receiving information. So a television show would be a great way to get a picture of the things I am discussing.
