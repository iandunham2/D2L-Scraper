While reflecting on how podcasting can fit into my career path I realize there are possibilities than I thought. The obvious route would be working directly in the podcasting industry, whether that is hosting, editing, or producing for a podcasting production company. The skills I have gained like audio editing, content planning, and audience engagement strategies would make me a strong candidate for those role.
However, I want to think beyond the traditional paths. The skills involved in  podcasting, interviewing, storytelling, and content development would be extremely valuable in other areas I am interested in such as marketing, public relations, and media production. In today’s job market, employers are constantly looking for candidates who know how to create engaging digital content and connect with audiences across platforms. Being able to conceptualize, produce, and promote a podcast demonstrates a versatile skill set that could help me stand out in a competitive field. 
While hosting my own podcast it opened door I hadn't considered before. It allowed me to build a personal brand through podcasting which could lead me to freelance opportunities. While also navigating the cooperate world these tools  are just as valuable. They can help me create compelling campaigns, present ideas  more effectively  and build stronger connections with companies or clients. Overall podcasting isn't just a side skill, it could become powerful tool in shaping my professional future whether I am working independently or within a larger organization.
