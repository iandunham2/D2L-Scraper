For my fourth episode, including research seems difficult for the academic source. My podcast is about college basketball, so finding a peer-reviewed article relevant to a topic I want to discuss is my fear. I trust that there will be something in the Kennesaw State University library database because anytime I have done academic research, I have had luck finding an article. As for the other two forms of research to include; I have what seems like an infinite amount of resources at my disposal. College basketball is entering the playoff time period, known as March Madness so all sports related journalistic sites or people are discussing seeding, winners, and teams who are not going to make it. The same goes for YouTube providing me the multimedia source for my video as there are other credible options to reference in my podcast. As for positioning each spot to mention the outside sources will take some practice and reshoots. I love introducing my own statistical research, but it comes, at its foundation, from other places so I can look at scores, outcomes, etc. Eager to put all of this into practice, and I know research is important for gaining trust so I would like to nail this!
