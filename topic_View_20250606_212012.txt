 A podcast I've been really into for a long time is The Athletic Football Show. What I find most appealing about its format is that it's made of different styles of episode each week, from a preview/recap of that week's games, to talking about what the draft might look like, interviews with coaches and current or former players, and covering different narratives that have popped up throughout the NFL season. Even during the offseason they manage to put out consistent episodes and remain interesting (to me anyways).
I hope to get a better understanding of using audio production software and how to create a podcast people will want to listen to. I don't really have any experience with production software so I want to get that experience from this. I'm mostly interested in writing, but the idea of a podcast is something I've been interested in for a while given that I'm really into sports and it's such a perfect field for podcasts. I think understanding how to put together a podcast that's cohesive in the way it's put together could be really beneficial for a future career in the realm of sports media.
