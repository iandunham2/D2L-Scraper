For those who may not know, I have a podcast labeled Hoopers Only. It is essentially about the sport basketball and leagues that involves basketball. Primarily the most famous leagues that involves basketball like the NBA and NCAA. I have yet to incorporate any sound effects in any of my episodes, but I know I can incorporate various sounds. Sounds such as basketball dribbling, basketball nets swooshing, basketball goal rim clanking and etc. 
On Episode 6, I'm thinking about taking a step back from the present and go into the past. I want to finish watching Court of Gold, the documentary of the basketball tournament for the 2024 Olympics. I have already thought of specific sounds that I could implement that would be effective. For this special episode, I could use NBC's Olympic theme song. When talking about Curry, I could use NBA's legendary analyst, Mike Breen's, infamous "bang" shout. I'm also thinking about going back and clipping audio from the Olympic Gold Medal game between Team USA and Team France. I have only watched the first two episodes of the documentary and have heard a couple of good key moments that I can record. For instance, there is already an iconic segment that features Anthony Edwards talking to former President, Barrack Obama, like he is a regular pedestrian on the street. It is hilarious.
