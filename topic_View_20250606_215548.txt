I've never been a fan of anything chaotic, chatty or gossipy when it comes to podcasts. When there are multiple hosts speaking over one another, I tend to feel stressed. When someone goes off on a tangent, and it feels unfiltered or out of control it takes away from what I like to experience from a podcast which is educational content, topics with depth or ways to be more relaxed. As a busy mom, our household is always crazy. When I turn to a podcast I am generally looking for a few minutes to myself to do something that can take me out of being in constant go-mode. When a podcast has a clear focus and direction it makes me feel like I am accomplishing a lesson and am able to keep interest. I chose the podcast Call Her Daddy. On this podcast, the host keeps things moving, however to me it feels almost as if you are stuck in a friend group and not allowed to chime in. I imagine they have to have a structured production process and I would never discredit someone's work due to their content, it always takes time to line up guests or to prepare what you will speak about on your episode. I think with a structure you can still go-with-the-flow or even sometimes come up with a phenomenal episode just because of the chemistry of the participants. I feel like the podcaster prepared just as much as the next person but knows and feels confident that they would be able to pivot if needed. These types of podcasts make me feel less fulfilled when I am done listening to them and I want to find some other type of media to recover from them. I know these styles have a big following, I would be interested to know how many of their followers are parents that have other things distracting them or overstimulating them daily.
