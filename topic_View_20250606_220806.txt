Sound affects me in many aspects of my life. I hear sounds around me whether its conscious or subconscious. For example, sometimes I hear birds chirping outside, but I am not always paying attention to the sound. I get a lot of information and entertainment through sound. I focus better when I cannot hear distractions. While saying that, I prefer to have something to listen to if I am by myself. I often have a show on in the background when I clean or listen to music when I drive. There have also been periods of time when I don't want to listen to a form of streaming audio. It can start to feel like a constant stream of noise if I am always listening to sounds all day long. 
If I could not hear, I would have to adjust certain habits that I have. I could see a few benefits. For example, my cats would not wake me up from meowing early in the mornings. I would also not be bothered by really loud noises. While there would be a few perks, it would still be a big challenge. I would have to learn to read sign language and develop new ways to receive information. While I could still read, I would not be able to process audio whether it comes from a voice in person or a voice through streaming audio. The forms of entertainment that I enjoy would be altered. I would not be able to hear audio in movies, music, and podcasts. It's important to not that the ability to hear sounds for granted. Sounds is crucial is the way that information is conveyed, entertainment is provided, and communication is processed. While sound is something that people may not always consciously pay attention to, it provides much more meaning to our lives.
