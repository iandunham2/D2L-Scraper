I think the possibilities of integrating a podcast into my career is endless. I believe it is endless because I have many different paths in the communication field that I wish to pursue. Integrating a podcast could open more doors than I could imagine. For instance, I have a podcast where I talk solely about basketball. If I evolve and elevate my skills over time, my podcast could possibly lead to me getting a job as an announcer or sports analyst. Being a sports anchor or analyst for ESPN or TNT is something I have always thought about doing. What's actually funny to me is the fact that when I was little, I wanted to be a World Wrestling Federation commentator. Now it is the WWE (World Wrestling Entertainment), but I never did close the door for that option of becoming a commentator for that organization. By incorporating episodes of me commentating on WWE matches, it could turn into an opportunity for me to work for them. Just look at Pat Mcfee for an example. You just never know who is watching. 
I would also like to point out that my podcast's topic is not the only thing that could help me with securing a position in my career. My audio editing skills could lead to me working as an audio technician for a crew in various companies. There could be an executive, from a company like Disney, that likes my voice. My voice alone, could help me obtain a voice acting job at a company. Voice acting is one thing I have always wanted to do. Then there is production. The way I format and produce my podcast could be a pivotal and crucial key into me being considered for a production gig. That is because they may like how I make things visual.
