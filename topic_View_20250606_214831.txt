The reading offered many people to pick and choose from when it comes to their identity shining through the narrative or text. Ultimately, being a huge history buff, I chose former President Barack Obama because of his small-time, relatable experience when growing-up and shaping his identity. President Obama was a child of a single parent household where he had to develop his own sense of what it meant to be a man because he did not have a father figure present in his life. His name alone also lets people think of his race and how important of an achievement it was for him to be the first African American President of the United States of America. Such an interesting idea that a president can have similar life experiences, but they translate to such different personality traits, ethnicities, etc.
As for the episode of the WTF podcast by Marc Maron I am choosing to highlight in this discussion; I am choosing Ke Huy Quan's episode because of the implications in his identity formation through fleeing Vietnam and becoming famous in the United States of America. Ke Huy Quan was a child-star in the classic films The Goonies and Indiana Jones. This is what blew him up as a celebrity and made him identifiable to the public; however, this is not who he truly is because he has his own experiences, thoughts, and an unique upbringing that molded him into who he is today. He reflects back on the moments that made him who he is and how the past translates to now since he has changed and blown up in popularity again. As for Marc Maron, his interview style almost seems like he is already great friends with the people, at least to the point where you are learning deeper things about your friend. To our ears it appears like he is questioning, but more resembles a conversation or a "remember when" session that most people can relate to nowadays.
