For my next podcast episode, I want to get more creative with the sound. Usually, I just focus on the script or interview questions, but after going through the chapter, I realized there’s a lot more I can do to make the episode better and more fun to listen to. I’m planning to go for a more comedic vibe and include meme sounds or audio clips that K-pop fans would recognize. I think it could make the episode feel more entertaining and relatable.
I also want to add in sound effects, kind of like how people edit YouTube videos. Things like funny reaction sounds or dramatic noises to highlight certain moments. It just makes the whole thing feel more alive and engaging.
One thing I really related to from the chapter was the part about not cutting out every single pause. I always debate with myself when I’m editing. Sometimes the pause feels natural, but I end up deleting it because I think I’m supposed to. Now I want to try leaving some of those moments in when it feels right. Overall, I think these changes will help me make a more dynamic and interesting episode.
