For me, streaming audio has highly affected my listening habits and has a major impact on my day to day. I love music and I listen to it all the time. Although, this can have a negative affect. Since I also love to make music, constantly listening to other music can hinder my own creative process, causing me to be too caught up in my influences at the current moment rather than just creating something that is stemming from me and the creativity that represents me. There will be times when I have to go days where I try to listen to as little outside music as possible so that when I create, I am able to make something without any commercial thought or influence. I find that when I am listening to music all the time (due to the convenience of streaming services) and creating during the same times, I adapt a natural commercial mindset to my own music, wondering if this track I'm making can be sent out to a certain artist, or hope that it has mainstream elements to it to be more widely perceived. In moments when I am listening to less outside music, I tend to have a much more free mind when it comes to creating, and I end up making something that I am ten times more passionate about than before. It took my a while to realize this. When I was younger and making beats I always thought that always listening to others and gaining influence was good, and it is to a certain extent, but having periods of time when you are only listening to your own music or podcasts as well instead of outside music bring your own creativity out. I think streaming audio and the convenience of it make us think that we always need to be playing music, but sometimes taking breaks from music and putting on a podcast or breaks from audio all together is a great thing.

I do think listening to just natural noise can be really beneficial to creativity too. Sometimes, it's just nice to hear the birds or gurgle of a creek. I do think that if we listen to audio too much than we don't learn to appreciate the silence, if that makes sense.
