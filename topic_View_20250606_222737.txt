While doing this assignment, I realized something. All of the podcasts I mainly watch don't have a music intro, outro, or break really. It is weird now that I think of it. What makes it more strange is I realized a couple of years back that a lot of YouTube content creators have dropped their intro theme music. I mainly watch and listen to basketball podcasts ( Club520, KG Certified, It Is What It Is, etc).  so that did not surprise me that those did not have musical intros. What took me by surprise is the only one podcast I watched about music, did not have a musical intro, break, or outro. That podcast is The Joe Budden Podcast. The host, Joe Budden, is a former rapper so for him to not have any of those essential things is wild to me.
The one podcast I listen to or watch weekly, KG Certified, implements a musical intro comprised of clips from past episodes mixed into a custom song by a DJ. The first thing that I noticed about how the music intro is effective, is the old soundbites in the song may make a newer viewer want to check out an older episode to hear the rest of what was said. The second thing is the music intro cues viewers/listeners for when the conversation is officially about to start. The style of music is a mixtape-sounding piece of music, which mixtapes and basketball have always gone hand-in-hand with each other. Mostly because you'd be surprised how many basketball players have their own song or mixtape. Its placement is strategetic because it is placed right after everybody has just arrived and is finally sitting down ready to work.
Link: https://podcast.apple.com/us/podcast/kg-certified/id1681173392?i=1000701246230
