For me personally, sound is a essential aspect of how I navigate the world. I believe that sound helps you make a mental connection with experiences. But it also shapes your experiences within life, sound distinguishes tone, mood, and emotion.  If I were deaf I think I would have a very hard time experiencing the same quality of life, because I personally rely very heavily on sound to process my surroundings and communications, but I also believe that sound forces us to become connected within the world. 
If I had only experienced live audio, I think I'd experience it in a more intense way. There wouldn't be the ability to rewind a song or conversation. It would all be in the moment which would require me to be present, at the risk of not being able to hear it again. But I also think I would really wish for the ability to record or rewind moments that you want to capture. 
Growing up I used to have an ipod touch and would listen to music all the time to escape certain realities that I was experiencing. I listened to just about everything country, rap, r&b and etc. It has played a very huge part in life because of the way sound has been able to transfer through headphones and can make anyone feel something, even through the midst of tragedy. Once I started streaming it has expanded the way I listen to music on other platform. But I also pay attention to the quality of what I am listening to now and I may also not always choose music for entertainment, now I lean more towards podcast. It has also made me pay attention to quality of content that I consume, I look for podcast that give me a little but of variety, when telling stories. It has also made music way more accessible but sometimes I feel like I don't appreciate it as much because there is always something new to hear.
