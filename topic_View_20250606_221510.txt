For this podcast, I want to focus on a conspiracy theory that was actually proven to be true. I figured this would give me more evidence to cite from for this specific episode. Once I figure out which conspiracy theory to cover, I'm going to need to do extra research to understand where the theory came from. I plan to do a generic search on Google to get a basic understanding of the conspiracy theory. Then, I will search for more professional articles to give me more details and missing pieces. Google Scholar and the KSU library database are my favorite resources to use. They're easy to navigate, and I almost always find the information that I am looking for. I tend to only read research articles, but this time I will look through books and other online resources. This research will be used in two ways. First, it will help me gain a better understanding of the topic. Once I have a better understanding, I will be able to write a better script. Second, the research I find will give me credibility. If I have the right sources to back me up, then the audience will trust what I am saying.
