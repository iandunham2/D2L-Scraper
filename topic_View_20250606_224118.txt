For Episode 6 of my podcast where I discuss my experiences living with Mitochondrial disorder, POTS, EDS, and Gastroparesis, I want to incorporate add different sounds that reflect moments of difficulty. An example such as the subtle sound of medical equipment or the sound of a heartbeat monitor which will help set the tone and give a sense of context to the struggles I discuss. What I also want to put sound effects to emphasize specific moments throughout the episode, like the feeling of  exhaustion or frustration. For example I could use a soft and fading sounds to show energy draining or the sensation of dizziness that will help to illustrate what I experience during flare-ups. These sounds will allow listeners to better understand the impact of these conditions beyond just the words. To help give the episode more depth I also plan to include on-site sounds if I discuss any visits to medical professionals. Background noises like a waiting room environment or the quiet conversations in a doctor's office can help set the scene and make listeners in the experience. By adding these elements I hope to make the episode more emotional offering listeners a more deep connection to the challenges I face with my health.
