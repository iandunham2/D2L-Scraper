For the fourth episode of my podcast, I’ll take a mix of academic, journalistic, and audio/video sources to make sure I have solid research while keeping things engaging.
For academic sources, I’ll check out Google Scholar, JSTOR, and university databases to find peer-reviewed articles and books that give deeper insights into my topic. These will help me add expert analysis and historical context, which I’ll break down in a way that makes sense for listeners.
For journalistic sources, I’ll look at The New York Times, The Korea Herald, and Billboard for recent articles, industry trends, and expert opinions. These will help me keep the discussion relevant and tied to real-world events. I’ll summarize key points and include direct quotes when they add something important to the conversation.
For audio and video sources, I’ll check out other podcasts, YouTube interviews, and documentaries to get firsthand perspectives from people in the industry. If an idol or producer shares something insightful, I’ll reference their words and build on that in my discussion.
Bringing all of these sources together will help make the episode well-researched without feeling like a lecture. My goal is to keep it informative but still fun and easy to listen to!
