The first sound I think of to add to my podcast would be sound effects like audience cheers, a quarterback calling a play, and crowd boos. Since my podcast is sports-related this would all help reinforce the atmosphere of it and give it a "sporty" vibe. Along with that, since it is a more narrative-focused podcast telling sports stories rather than just talking about players and news in the sports world, music to set the tone during stories would also help a lot. There are plenty of emotional sports stories so having some music that is a bit more somber could be useful, and then having some music that sounds more triumphant sounding when some odds are overcome, a player makes a game-saving play, or a team finally accomplishes something they never have before. It is specifically football that I'll be talking about so some other sounds could be the sound of players colliding, sound clips from specific plays that happened and the reactions to it, or even soundbites from players during the particular game since a lot of players have mics that they wear during games.
