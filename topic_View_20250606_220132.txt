In this discussion I will focus on a podcast that is part of the rambling discussion/ dramatized content because I do not listen to that genre of podcasts. If I do listen to a podcast it is definitely sports commentary or scientific research as those are what I am interested in. The podcast I am choosing is the "H3 podcast". H3 is a popular brand with a large podcast following. I knew of them and the content; however, since I do not like or listen to that content I always avoid the podcasts H3 released. They definitely are a rambling one because they will just talk about life experiences but usually political affairs, and touchy subjects seem to pop in. Upon viewing an episode and watching some clips of others. My reaction is that it reaffirms my belief, at least with H3's podcast, that I do not rambling, life experiences podcasts (at the moment). They seem to be very eager to be apart of "cancel culture" an aspect that seemed like they thrive on drama and controversy. The comedy did not really hit with me personally. Possibly because I am not the target audience? As for the production, even if I do not like H3's podcast I do believe they prepare, edit, and have an extended process in uploading an episode. They have a larger production team outside of the host(s) who will also relay some thoughts when provoked. It does seem more livestreamed than prerecorded and massively edited. Overall, enjoyed the experience in trying a different podcast, but maybe I should have tried one I knew I did not have some initial knowledge about. H3 did not personally pass for me. Music reviews would be the next genre for me to try because I would not do political commentaries as I find the topics stressful.
