To expand the feeling and mood of The Prepped Mama podcast, I would consider crafting sound to fulfill the urgent need of being prepared. Layering sounds of nature but also intense or tense patterns of things that could potentially feel threatening but not scared. Since this podcast is based on preparedness it's important to emphasize why you should be prepared and the need to be prepared, however that is hard to get across without making someone feel scared. I want listeners to feel at ease, I might use things such as news clips at time or bytes that talk about natural disaster or things that people are more comfortable hearing about because they are common occurrences. Bad storms, trees cracking, tornado sirens or other disaster based sounds are hard to craft in a way that is "ambient" but I think it can be done with intentional choices and the right tools. I think good additions could be the wind blowing, trees rustling, children playing but fading out into a serious choice to make somehow to emphasize that the choices we make to be prepared aren't only for us but also for our children and little ones that need us. I would like to create a sense of urgency to be prepared but not a scared fear mongered vibe.
