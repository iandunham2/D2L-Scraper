For my last podcast episode, I want to do something a little different. It would be like having five mini episodes in one. So, at the beginning of this school year my mentor told me to write down five truths about God that I could cling to throughout the school year. I wrote these truths down and they have been on my mirror all school year. That said, I think it would be cool to go through what these truths are and talk about how God has had them play out in my life this past school year. I think that the opportunity to film a video for this last episode is super cool and fun, however, I do not think that I will have the capacity to do so next week. Even though I am deciding not to do have a visual element to my podcast I still want to put a lot of time into planning my episode and editing it. This would look like me planning and filming my episode earlier in the week and slowly editing it throughout the week to make sure I have a good finished product. I really want to end this semester and my podcast on a strong note and to end with something that I am proud of because this class has taught me so much!
