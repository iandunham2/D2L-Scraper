https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1gQ8fyUOVuE&ab_channel=VeryReallyGood
For this week, I decided to go with a podcast I've become really entertained by lately, which is Kurtis Conner's "Very Really Good" podcast. This podcast has a very retro stylization to it, with grainy graphics like you would see on an old tv, as well as the actual set of the podcast full of vintage toys, furniture, and colors. I really enjoy the aesthetic of the podcast, especially since it's very different from the usual YouTube videos I watch of Kurtis'. The music for the intro of the podcast is very reminiscent of a jazz bar piano, which is easy on the ears but also enticing to listeners as they begin the episode. It almost reminds me of the music they play at the end of each episode of Saturday Night Live, a lively and bold piano that screams 70's to me. In this episode, there is no ad-read or break music, which I thought was interesting as most big podcasts do have ads or sponsorships, but maybe it was just a one off thing. For the outro, it's a similar music style, maybe the other half of the piano track that plays at the beginning of the episodes. Overall, I think the music choice is great for the podcast, as it is fun and jazzy, but also calm enough for the setting of the podcast, where Kurtis is not making some big production, just talking about what's going on in his bubble of online media that week.
