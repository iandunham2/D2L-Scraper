This class has been going very well for me this semester and it has been teaching me a lot. I feel as though the stuff that is teaching me the most is really just completing the podcasts. I think just going in and being hands on will always teach the most, but I also do really like how the podcasts are every other week instead of every week. It keeps things fresh and makes it easier to find news to talk about to make the podcast a good one. I feel like the main challenge was just learning Resolve. I have experience in a few other programs for music production and video editing, so a lot of stuff carried over, but learning a new program is always going to come with a different learning curve. Overall, the class has been amazing and has had a great mix of learning and also fun involved with the learning.
