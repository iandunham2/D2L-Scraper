I think my podcast would benefit from having clips of the media I am discussing each week in the episodes. For example, when I discussed the impact of The Wizard of OZ on the queer community, I think that adding in clips from the songs, as well as clips of Judy Garland speaking that would have helped to expand the episodes and create a better overall production. I also think this would help in terms of sound effects which I would like to add in for a type of comedic effect when I'm talking about references in the LGBT+ community that listeners may not understand by me just saying it. In one of my episodes, I reference a a clip of Lady Gaga on Jimmy Kimmel talking about only wanting a child if they were gay. Out of context this sounds really harsh, and it's hard to explain the clip without the soundbite, so I think that adding context to what exactly you are talking about so the listener can understand what the reference sounds like and they can understand the comedic context behind it, that also helps your podcast out. Sound effects overall not only help with context though, as they also provide an aesthetic ambiance to some parts of your podcast if you so choose to have that, like a soft piano whilst you're reading a quote, or playing some sort of nature sound as you're describing a certain part of your story can help immerse the audience even deeper.

Hi Makenzie,
I love the idea of adding media clips to your podcast! It would definitely help bring the references to life and make the episodes more engaging. Some things, like Judy Garland’s impact or Lady Gaga’s humor, just don’t hit the same when they’re only explained rather than heard. Plus, using soundbites for comedic effect is such a smart move—sometimes tone and delivery make all the difference in understanding a joke or reference.
And yeah, adding background sounds or music can really set the mood. A soft piano behind a quote could make it feel more profound, while nature sounds or other ambient effects could make certain moments feel more immersive. It’s those little details that can really elevate a podcast from just being informative to being an experience. Can’t wait to hear how it turns out!
