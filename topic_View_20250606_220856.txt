I don't think I recognized the effect that sound has on my experiences until I really thought deeply about it. I grew up with all types of media and streaming services, as well as radio and podcasts and fast access to music. Whether I am listening to music at practice or a game, or listening to podcasts while I cook or clean, I have always been someone who likes to have something playing in the background. If I were deaf, I think my relationship with the media would be much different than it is now. Sound allows media to have its full effect and tell a story, so without it I feel like I would be missing pieces. For watching videos and films, I think I would have to rely much more on subtitles and visual cues to be able to recognize what is happening. It is so crazy how much more you can process from a film by understanding tone of voice or background music. I also considered that I would think live music was extremely special because I would have never heard recorded audio before. Feeling the vibrations of live music and never hearing the same thing again would be such a memorable experience. With how simple it is so skip around and listen to whatever music I want, I guess I haven't considered the value of all the music I listen to fully.
I have a new understanding for how grateful I am that I have the ability to listen to the things I love. Even as I am sitting in this coffee shop I can hear the movements and steps of people around me, the laughter and connection, and even the sound of my keyboard. Noise is such a gift that I hope I don't take for granted any longer.
