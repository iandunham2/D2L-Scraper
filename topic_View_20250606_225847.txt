As the end of the podcast season approaches, I would like to include a look back on everything discussed through the episodes. I want to to highlight things that were some of my favorites to talk about as well as things I want to do more in the future like bringing other guests on to talk about their favorite queer media, their experiences as a queer person, etc. I also would like to talk about my favorite piece of queer media that I had saved for the final episode so I could end on my most positive note. I think that integrating video into my production process would be interesting for me, as when I usually record my podcasts I don't look "camera ready" most of the time. I would have to think about my outfit, my "set", and other things within the aesthetic side that I thankfully haven't had to worry about previously. For my final episode, I think I want to out everything I have learned this season to the test and make sure it truly is my best work that I could showcase proudly to my friends, family, and even potential employers on my resume. Overall, I think the final episode should be a farewell to what this class has helped me learn as far as editing something that seemed so daunting a few months ago, as well as maybe a "see you later" to the content I still have so much talking about to do.
