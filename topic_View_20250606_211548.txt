Honestly, I am not huge into podcasts and have never really become attached to any specific one. If I were to really dive into podcasts (which I plan to), I would say I enjoy podcasts that teach you new information rather than podcasts simply meant for entertainment. Another type of format I would say I enjoy is when two people are involved in an episode, whether its an interview format or just with two hosts. I feel like listening to two or more people converse is a lot more interesting and involved than listening to just one person. 
When it comes to this class, I'm really excited to learn more about the process of trying to make podcasts sound as crispy as possible. I produce music and have dabbled in vocal mixing when in sessions and I've always found it very interesting. I think this class will give me a totally different perspective on audio mixing skills and will possibly simplify my thought process when it comes to vocal mixing in the future. I also am just hoping to get a better understanding of the podcast workflow. I feel like skills in this field will help me when I graduate and start my career with my major. Very excited for this semester and especially this class!
