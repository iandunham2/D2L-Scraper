My podcast is about reviewing new movie releases. A lot of times in my episodes, I tell the audience the story of the particular movie I am reviewing. I think I can incorporate sound effects into my podcast episodes when I am telling the story of the movie I am reviewing that week. The sound effects I would use all depends on what type of movie I am reviewing which could range from horror to comedy. In the past, I had never really put much thought into adding sound effects into my podcast because, I had never really listened to podcasters who did that before. But, upon finding podcasters who add sound effects into their podcasts I can definitely see how it makes for a more interesting listening experience. Moreover, after the reading for this week it also gave me inspiration on how I can incorporate sound effects into my episodes. For example, if the movie I am discussing is particularly dramatic I could always add in some dramatic effects to emphasize the part of the story I am going over. But, if the movie I am discussing is sad, I could add in some somber sound effects to emphasize those emotions within the listener. Overall, I think adding sound effects into my podcast episodes will improve the audience's listening experience and keep them more engaged.
