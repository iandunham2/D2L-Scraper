 https://open.spotify.com/episode/2iuVjCu9yWOAxW87hUZT0U?si=Kwf2_8lRTX-VqAXPcIc2YQ
This is a Joe Rogan podcast where he has on Daryl Davis, the first black man who was a member of the KKK. This interview was compelling for multiple reasons, Joe Rogan was asking good questions and this story was also just super interesting. The podcast is a little over 2 and a half hours long, some might think this is too long but the episode never got boring in my opinion and everything he said was important to the podcast. The way Darly Davis tells stories is also another reason that this episode is so good. He knows how to paint a word picture and keep listeners engaged. It seems like Joe Rogan doesn't really have a list of questions but rather just topics and points that he knows he wants to get to so he can get the podcast flowing better. I think that if he had a a list of questions that he stuck to then the interview would feel less personal and would take away all of the little anecdotes that are added in by the flow of the episode. I would say the most important moment in this podcast would be the first question Joe asks to get the interviewee to start talking and feel comfortable telling his story.
