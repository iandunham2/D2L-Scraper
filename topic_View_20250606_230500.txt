I ultimately want to be a published author and having a successful podcast could be a good way to build my platform and make me more marketable for publishers. Though in my podcast I want to expand my podcast to include more of what I want instead of just what this class requires. I'll still keep in the niche, but I also like doing commentary on popular movies and TV shows as well, which could help me trend easier. I'd also like to expand into books as well. Not that my podcast is anything great, but I do like starting conversation about the Christian conservative film industry since it does seem to be swept under the rug and not taken seriously, which is very unfair since so many other indie films get attention.
Skills that come with producing a podcast can be useful for any job such as attention to detail, careful planning, and editing skills. For each episode that I make, I research the podcast by watching the films and looking up news around the films and then I write a script, all before recording. I could use these skills to help write podcasts for a company even if I'm not the one hosting. Oftentimes, a podcaster will have a team that does the research and writing for them while they read off what is written for them. Podcast are very popular and everyone wants to jump onto the bandwagon.
I'm not sure exactly where my career will take me, but I have potential job with my fiance's dad who owns a writing company and if a client wants me to write podcast then I can tell them that I have the skills to do so and can talk intelligently about the structure of the podcast and the details of how to write a good one.
