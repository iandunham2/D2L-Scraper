This class has been fine this semester. I have loved getting to explore Resolve and learning how to actually make a good podcast. I think my editing and podcasting skills are definitely improving after each episode. I will say the most challenging part is going back in and editing out a lot of filler words. That part can take a while depending on how many I used. I think the assignment that was the most useful was the interview episode as it taught me how to structure an episode in a way that makes sense and it was fun to talk to someone else about my topic and see how their views differ or were similar from mine.
