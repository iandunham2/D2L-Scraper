For this episode of my podcast I went about finding my evidence in pretty simple ways. I wrote my script before the episode and decided what sources I wanted to use based on the segments I was going to talk about. For the academic journal I just looked up a deep dive into the film that I was talking about and read a few till I found something interesting, I decided to do the New Yorker because it had a cool analysis of the film and made sense in the segment. For the academic source I went to the KSU library online and looked up masculinity in wrestling , this source wasn’t about the movie in specific but I wanted to use research on this topic because it is a big theme in the film so it would be cool to do research and apply things that aren’t already said about the film. For the video section I looked up an interview with the director because I wanted to discuss the actors and how they dedicated themselves to their roles so I just looked up Sean durkin iron claw interviews on YouTube and it came up. This was harder because it wasn’t something I could just read off.
