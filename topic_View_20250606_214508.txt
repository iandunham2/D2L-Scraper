Module 5: Good Interviews
Perry Holliday posted Feb 9, 2025 9:02 PM
Subscribe 
 
 
 
 
 Subscribed
   
 
 In the Envelope is an interview podcast focused on acting. Host Vinnie Mancuso is the Senior Editor at Backstage, and he interviews well known actors on the podcast. The interviews provide the listeners with more knowledge about the process of getting acting jobs. The most recent interview is with award winning actress Demi Moore. Each episode is about 30-45 minutes long. I enjoy longer interviews, so I feel like they are too short. I also think there would be more episodes being published. However, this is a very reasonable interview time and each episode provides plenty of information. The interviewer is professional and well prepared. Because the podcast revolves around acting, he is always up to date on what each guest has worked on in terms of films. I think it's important to take the conversation a little more seriously for this type of podcast. It's important to not waste the guests' time. The most important moment would probably be the end of each episode. After the actor being interview finishes, they leave the listeners with a piece of advice. Often, their advice reflects their journey they went through. After listening the the entire interview, the advice is always more meaningful.
