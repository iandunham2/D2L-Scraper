In my podcast, I would say most of the sound effects I could add would just come in the intro, outro, and breaks. One idea I have off the top of my head is to add sounds of people cheering and tennis sounds like a ball being hit or an umpire stating the score of a match. It sounds cheesy but again I think that "cheesy" element allows for a high level of remembrance among the audience. People cling onto stuff like that and begin to associate it with your podcast. Other sound effects I can think of is adding a sound before I listen back to another audio clip, like a record sound on a camera or something like that. Another sound effect would possibly be risers and other sound effects in that realm that could be used before transitions and leaving transitions. That would add another element to making things flow smoothly throughout the podcast. I would want to be careful not to add too many sounds because I think it is easy to add too many sounds thinking it sounds good but to other listeners there is no flow to it. Overall, I think the use of sound effects could really improve my podcast as a whole.
