Podcast Format:
I love the podcast Basically Unfiltered because of its  very casual laid-back vibe where the hosts (Zane, Heath, Alisha, and Remi) just sit down and talk like friends. The chemistry between them feels natural and authentic with each episode feeling more like an unfiltered conversation rather than a scripted show. The mix of humor, real-life stories, and candid opinions makes it relatable and easy to connect with. The way they switch between lighthearted moments and deeper more thoughtful discussions keeps things engaging, and the variety of topics they cover makes it feel fresh every time.
Goals for This Class:
In this class, I’m hoping to develop better production skills especially in editing and mixing audio to create a clean professional sound. I want to understand the technical side of podcasting better from equipment setup to post-production. I’m also excited to learn how to organize and manage a podcast from start to finish ensuring the workflow is efficient and smooth. Ultimately I want to leave with a polished podcast, gaining confidence in both my production abilities and my ability to craft engaging content.
