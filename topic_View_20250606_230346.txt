I am so thankful this class required me to build an entire season of podcast episodes. The skills it allows for me to demonstrate on multiple occasions greatly increase my chances of landing a career in the media and entertainment business! What type of career could that mean? At the moment I do not know what career I would like to go into, but the podcasts are an excellent addition to my digital portfolio or my professional social media platforms. This allows for future employers to see some credible experience on essentially my resume. I am most excited about that aspect because I did not take an internship during my college career. These projects like the podcast are the replacement for the internship I believe. I mentioned skills that I learned and grew because of making the podcast. Attention to detail, consistency, knowledge, and topic itself are aspects I believe employers can see/ hear when listening to my podcast thus making it more likely that I can receive a job in something like social media coordinator, editor, sports host, etc. Additionally, knowing I am capable of producing an entire show myself allows for me to grow my personal brand that could turn into something that makes money and is my career is never out of the cards. It is something my generation is lucky to say they can do! I relate this to streamers, influencers, YouTubers, etc. In the end, I would totally be interested in a career that is all about podcasting depending on the topic, my range of creativity, and of course pay. Right now, my podcasts are not paying any bills which will not cut it, but who knows maybe I am the next podcast host for CBS sports discussing all things college basketball because of this podcast!
