With the first source having to be a peer reviewed academic journal, I will be researching on the KSU library database to find some topics on tennis. I feel like a lot of the peer reviewed sources related to tennis will have to do with the mental side of tennis as well as the physical side. I plan on trying to find an academic journal on tennis players' mental abilities while on court. For the journalistic source, I will look up the latest headlines in the sport. I already covered the largest recent headline regarding Jannik Sinner in last episode, so I'll have to find something different from that. There are a lot of headlines regarding tournaments this past week, such as the women's ATX Open in Austin, Texas, as well as the men's tournament in Acapulco, Mexico. When it comes to the last media related source, I will likely find a video talking about the upcoming tournament in Indian Wells, California. This is a huge tournament, and I will likely start the podcast giving a preview to the tournament and integrating the media that I find. I normally split the topics of my podcasts into three different sections, so the three different pieces of research will be easily implemented into three different topics.
