For the next episode of my podcast " The Generational Gap" I plan to incorporate sound design to keep the listener immersed in the podcast with tiny unexpected sound. While studying sound design in my former class I learned how important sound can make up the experience of a movie, show, podcast, and etc.  Even in my content that I film and edit on my free time, you can notice a huge difference when sound effects are being used vs when they aren't. I have seen data and even experience higher views and more engagement when incorporating sound design. However, you can't just use any sound, the choice of sound is also very important so that it matches the vibes of the podcast. I plan to use soft but effective sound effects in my podcast the vibe of my podcast is fun, comfy, conversational, so small sounds like typing keys, phone notifications, or relatable genz sounds. For Millennials’ nostalgia, I might add the sound of a dial-up modem or an old Nokia ringtone. It really depends on the topic that I will be discussing that will determine the sound, but with that in mind I also want the sound effects to help set the tone for the podcast episode.
