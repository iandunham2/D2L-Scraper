One person whose identity was visible through the narrative and stuck out to me was Laura Jane Grace. She talked about how hard it was for her to be ok with who she was as a trans woman. She tried to shut that part of her life out but it only got worse and harder to ignore. She said how you should own that part of you and that you should be confident and comfortable. That you should be who you are and not explain anything to anyone if you don't want to. 
I listened to the episode of the WTF podcast with Elizabeth Olsen. She talks about her time on set and how she got into acting after her sisters exploded to fame. One thing that stuck out to me was how she was explaining how she has two different journals that she journals in everyday. She has a personal journal and a character journal to kind of separate her life from. She talked about how she journals in her personal journal everyday and keeps her character journal for when she has a role coming up and she wants to get in character for it. Another thing that stuck out to me was when she decided that she wanted to be an actress. She didn't want to be a film or tv actor like her sisters but a theater actor. It was interesting when Elizabeth said that she wanted to be known for her craft and not just the fame or who her sisters are. She mentions how living in Los Angeles surrounded by family members and friends who were in the film industry really helped shape what she wanted and how to look at it. She didn't think of Hollywood as some magical place but instead thought of it as just a job. This made for a compelling podcast because I got to really know Elizabeth Olsen better and learn more about her as a person. She really is humble and treats her job and the people around her with respect which I feel like not all actors are like that. Marc's interview style was fun as he asked great questions and bounced from topic to topic with ease. I liked how he also made sure his guests were comfortable and made sure they were having a good time by making jokes and relating to his guests.
