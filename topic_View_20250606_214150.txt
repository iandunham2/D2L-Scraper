Subscribe

 For my "good" interview, I chose Gayle King's interview of R. Kelly on CBS.
This interview provides compelling content due to the context and lore behind R. Kelly. Before this interview, he had never sat down and publicly talked about the allegations at the time of him being a pedophile amongst other things. That sparked interest because he is one of the biggest music writers of all time and is dubbed the king of R&B. What made it more compelling was r. Kelly playing the victim of the situation.
The interview is rather long, with the final cut officially being 46 minutes long. I couldn't find the full interview so I linked a video to the highlights of it. I believe the interview would have ended up longer if it was not for him walking out.
Gayle King, the interviewer, has a formal style of view. That is what is expected due to the magnitude of this interview and the platform it takes place. This being a formal interview makes a big difference because it helps keep the interview organized for the most part, although the interview did feature a brief rant from R. Kelly.
The most important part of the interview, in my opinion, is when Gayle's camera crew kept the cameras rolling during R. Kelly's rant and she stayed calm and refocused the interview. I believe that is the most important moment because as an interviewer, you never know what question you ask might trigger or piss your interviewee off.
