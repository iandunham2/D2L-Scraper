Module 5: Good Interviews
Kiel Cole posted Feb 9, 2025 7:12 PM
Subscribe 
 
 
 
 
 Subscribed
   
 
 A great example of a good podcast interview (in my slightly biased opinion) is Eric Nam’s episode of The Daebak Show featuring TXT’s Yeonjun and Taehyun.
(link)
The interview is such an easy and fun listen. It feels more like a casual chat than a formal interview, which makes it really engaging. They share behind-the-scenes stories, joke around, and even open up about their careers, so you get a mix of fun and deeper moments.
At about 45 minutes, the length feels just right. If it were shorter, we’d miss out on some great moments, and if it were longer, it might start dragging. Eric has a relaxed, natural interview style. He’s not just reading off a list of questions. He reacts in the moment, plays off their energy, and makes them feel comfortable, which leads to some really funny and genuine conversations.
One of the best moments is when they talk about the pressures of being idols. They reflect on how much they’ve grown and what it’s like trying to balance their careers with their personal lives. It’s a cool reminder that even though they’re global stars, they’re also just young guys figuring things out like everyone else. For anyone interested in K-pop, I would recommend it.
