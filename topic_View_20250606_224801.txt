I chose The Minimalists Hedonism episode ( https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-minimalists/id1069757084 )The main question of this podcast is the pursuit of pleasure seeking preventing you from true happiness, This question begins a deep dive into hedonism where searching for instant results within gratification creates an emptiness in or body. The idea that hedonism will make you happy is something that people do not consider at their worst, if you are battered by consumerism and give in to these desires it can actual reflect psychopathic behavior - you start justifying the reasons you are doing this. Not caring about others and only about yourself or not caring about your future self can be essentially the same thing. You are literally punishing yourself for today's pleasures regardless of the outcomes. The hosts discuss what has been their guilty pleasure in the past with consumerism and creates a connection with the audience in this bigger discussion. 
Creating a discussion around material possessions creates a false perception of happiness. There is a big change in the concept from being over indulgent and moving into the results of your actions. The conversation makes the audience think of their own actions and if they are being over indulgent. 
The musical interludes have lyrics that directly reflect the goal of the podcast which is to make you aware of the possibility that you are over indulging in life in general and acquiring the things you don't need which can make you resentful of the decisions you make. The tone is light hearted but they include emotional cues and lyrics that make you wonder about your own behavior. 
There is a big role with editing in this podcast the natural conversation makes you feel that you are really just in the middle of a conversation but they don't talk over the top of each other which can sometimes feel chaotic with multiple hosts. It feels very seamless and still includes some humor while keeping the conversation moving and focused on the goal. When they bring in the listener comments or questions it feels very natural to even pull in that participant they have done a great job with keeping it all consistent and it doesn't feel like it is speeding but it definitely feels like it's moving. 
This podcast feels like it is a natural impromptu conversation but it is certainly scripted to allow for the amount of information they fit in.
