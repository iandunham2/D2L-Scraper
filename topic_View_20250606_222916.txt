For this discussion, I will being showcasing the podcast channel titled "NFR Podcast". I chose their podcast series because it is different then just incorporating the music into the podcast; instead, it is the topic of discussion. They focus on album reviews, best of whatever genre, and top artists. I like how the actual music becomes the forefront idea which allows for the listener to connect with the creators in the video. By promoting certain stances they achieve a greater chance of interaction with their followers or fans. NFR mainly focuses on hip-hop and rap from what I can tell which is the biggest genres of today's society if I have to say. Their podcasts generate so many "Oh, I forgot how good that song was." or "Oh, yeah that song was so annoying to listen to." moments. I would like my podcasts to generate experiences where the viewer/ listener feels engaged with the content as if they are present discussing certain stances in-person. Not necessarily the music helping me to determine the music I should include, but how to set-up podcast videos! The video I will link below is such an interesting concept to do a bracket/ playoffs of certain rap songs. I made my own along with the video and it is so entertaining to see how it matches up with the creators because music is such a polarizing yet mutual experience.
