 I like to listen to Celebrity Memoir Book Club. The podcast is hosted by New York comedians Claire Parker and Ashley Hamilton, and each episode they are reviewing celebrity memoirs. The hosts do a good job at providing comedic relief and giving honest reviews. They pick both relevant and random celebrity memoirs such as Brittney Spears, Jill Duggar, and Mike Sorrentino (Mike the Situation). I can't remember how I found the podcast, but it's always been one that I go back to for new episodes. If I am familiar with the celebrity's name or work, then I will probably end up listening. 
I hope to become more comfortable with making podcasts and other audio projects. I also want to become more familiar with editing software. I have very little experience with sound and audio, but I feel like this class will force me to learn all that I need to know. I do not have a large portfolio, so I hope to make podcasts episodes that are worthy of showing to employers. In the past, I tended to ignore the importance of audio. However, I am really excited to work on my recording and editing skills while also talking about interesting topics.
