The Serial Killers Spotify Podcast had good use of music throughout each episode. In the episode titled "the Rainbow Murders," mysterious music is used in the host's introduction to set the tone. Once the episode takes off, there is very subtle background music to go along with the story. It almost sounds like a fancy audiobook. As the story becomes more intense, the music grows with it. What I like most about the use of audio in the podcast is the way it is being used. Instead of using music to introduce or cut to breaks, the music is played at the same time as the host speaks. It's just the right volume level. It doesn't take away from the actual podcast host, nor does it serve little purpose. The episode flowed really smooth because of the audio. True crime is an interesting topic because on the one hand, it's supposed to be very informational. On the other hand, anyone telling a true crime story needs to have good storytelling abilities. Music is a factor of communication and can add a lot of feelings to stories. I think this podcast is a great reference in terms of using audio.
