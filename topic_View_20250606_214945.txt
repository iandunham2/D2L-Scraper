In this weeks reading we explore what identity looks like from various standpoints and how it can make up some people. One interview that stood out me was Barack Obama. Aside from him being the first black president he has had a abundance of identity challenges. He was raised in a single parent home and dealt with the challenges of not having a dad. He also has had a lot of cultural conflict between his Hawaiian culture and the African American culture.  Obama's journey reflects a broader struggle many individuals face in navigating multiple cultural identities and finding a cohesive sense of self. 
In Marc Maron’s interview with Ariana Grande on WTF, she talks about how her identity as a pop star, Italian-American woman, and former child actress has shaped her experiences in the industry. Ariana obviously grew up in the spotlight and how dealing with the public can be lot that can start to play part in the identity. One of the first things Ariana  talk about is her being vegan when she grew up with Italian parents when her culture looked like eating meats and cheeses. What makes this episode so interesting is that Maron creates a space where she can be open and real, beyond just the celebrity image people see. She talks about finding balance, staying authentic, and navigating an industry that constantly tries to define her. It’s a great example of how WTF explores identity in a way that feels raw and personal, making the conversation feel more like a deep, honest reflection rather than just another interview.
