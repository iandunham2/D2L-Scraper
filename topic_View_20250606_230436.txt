I really enjoyed learning how to make a podcast this semester, and though I would need to perfect my skills a lot it is something I would love to try to make a career out of. In all of the skills/ classes I have taken for Media and Entertainment, this is the one class where I found something that I enjoyed doing and became passionate about. I think that if I were to be able to make podcasting a career it could open doors for things like making merchandise, sponsorships, live events, and the creation of digital products for my listeners. A lot of these things are me dreaming big, however, I do believe that if I were to become successful in podcasting it could open these doors in my life. Specifically, I think it could be cool to make digital products for my audience. I have seen many people, especially in the Christian podcasting field, make things like planners or bible study guides to help their audience grow. Also, in terms of live events, I went to a conference this past year that was hosted by a couple of the Christian podcaster’s that I watch. This is something that I think is especially cool because this transcended a podcast and I got to be with their online community in real life. Also, I have gone to a podcast tour of a podcaster that I have watched as well. Overall, I think that it is cool that podcasting can open more cool opportunities for someone that is beyond creating a podcast itself. It can lead you to take all of these leaps of faith that I believe can really help others in their journeys and change lives! If it weren’t for a lot of the podcasts that I have watched or conferences I have been to from my favorite creators I do not think my faith would be where it is today, which is a testament to how amazing it can be to making podcasting a career!
