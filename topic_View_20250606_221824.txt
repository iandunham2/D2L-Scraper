As both generations grow and explore their own identities beliefs, values, morals began to become a big part of how they determine themselves. 
I will look for academic sources through the Kennesaw State Library database or any other academic journals.  I am pretty sure that it may be a little difficult finding article/peer review specifically about identity in library database, but I plan to use words like " social media, effects, psychological" my hope is that these key words bring up the effects of how certain situations have altered or changed the idea of how they may identify. For journal articles I will look more towards the New York Times, mainly looking for articles about the what research has shown about the two generations, whether it is about patterns or common triggers when it comes to identity.
