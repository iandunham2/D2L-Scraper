For the 4th episode of my podcast I researched Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS) and how it’s portrayed in the media but particularly focusing on Sia the singers own experience with the condition. I had started by looking up reputable sources like medical journals, EDS advocacy websites, and educational platforms like the Ehlers-Danlos Society to get different accurate information about the syndrome and different new things that have been discovered. I also look into personal stories or interviews from people with EDS as well as public interview done by Sia regarding her own experience with the condition.
To make sure the information I provide is reliable, I also check out YouTube videos, podcasts, or online forums where people with EDS have shared their experiences with the illness. I want to have different perspectives from those who live with the condition, as well as medical experts, to present a well-rounded view of EDS.
As for the research into my production I wanted focus on including factual details about the symptoms, challenges, and the impact of EDS on daily life but from real people. I might also discuss how Sia’s experience has sparked conversations about disability and chronic illness, especially in the context of her public persona and the media. I'll use quotes from interviews or articles to help humanize the topic and engage listeners with both personal and professional viewpoints. This research will help me create a thoughtful, informed, and engaging episode.
