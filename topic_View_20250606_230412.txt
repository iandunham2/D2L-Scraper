Podcasting as it relates to a possible path in my career is interesting. As I want to go into social media marketing, I think there are some ways I can definitely integrate skills from podcasting into my profession, but I don't think I would want to have podcasting as a full time job. Using tools like sound editing, mapping out content, and maybe even hosting a show in less of a podcast way and more of a web show way would be things I would like to use in my career. If I were to work in the podcasting industry, I think I would like to work not for myself but for a podcasting company like Unwell, Simplecast, or Anchor that have their entire companies and positions dedicated to parts of the podcasts they produce. Jobs in this like coming up with content/ topics for the hosts to discuss or marketing for the podcast would be things I would be interested in doing at a podcasting company versus being an actual host or editor which are both interesting jobs but I do not think I could do it in the long run as it feels like it would get to monotonous and boring for me after awhile. One thing I did enjoy that would be an interesting career that could have podcasting attached to it is the NPR episode we listened to about the Dollar Stores taking over lower income areas and the hosts actually went out an explored the areas and spoke with residents and local government about the issue. Going out in the world and experiencing it is what interests me the most, and I think if there was a proper way to turn this into a career, almost like a blog that I narrate into a podcast about the places I travel to and including the actual time of me being there in the audio, that would be a great career I would love to have.
