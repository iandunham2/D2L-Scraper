I have actually been an on-air talent for a podcast in the past and made decent money off of it. The experience was a good bit different than this class as this was all remote and I had to show up to a recording studio when I worked that job. I enjoyed working as a host on a podcast and being able to interact with guest stars. Truthfully, I would look to do it again if given the opportunity and a nicer commute than I had prior. The main difference here was that I was doing everything alone and also going based on a loose script rather than just focusing on sticking to topics and being able to just keep talking on and on. I actually prefer being able to just have a topic and be able to talk on and on about that topic or make interview questions up on the spot rather than sticking to a script as it feels more natural for me and lets me connect with people more. Going off a script or loose script and having to memorize facts about things restricts me so much from being able to have fun with a topic which made this class a struggle for me. 
Looking at other possible positions in podcasting, I actually would not mind working on the editing portion. I have always loved and enjoyed editing and would have zero issues looking into doing that full-time as long as it isn't my own voice. I love editing clips or audios for other people but typically tend to do the worst edits for myself as I rush through it due to my disdain for my own voice and just being overly self-conscious of what I am saying and how I am saying it. This is never an issue if I'm the just talent and not the editor as I don't have to listen to or think back on what I've said.
