This whole semester I have been talking about some of my favorite A24 films and my takes on them. As the semester has progressed, I have had to add more things to each episode, and now I feel like we are at a point where I can make my episodes way more immersive than they were at the beginning of the semester. For the last episode, I am breaking the rules just a little bit. I am supposed to only be talking about A24 films, but I just watched a movie that has become my favorite movie of all time. The movie is called The Elephant Man. I think this episode will be very good because I feel super passionate about this film, and I think the episode will flow very well because I have so much to say and a vision of how I want the podcast to look. I do not think I will add the visual aspect to my podcast, I feel as if there is no real need unless I just wanted to show certain clips from the movie, I feel this is a new skill I would need to learn so I don't throw of the flow that I have learned in these episodes.
