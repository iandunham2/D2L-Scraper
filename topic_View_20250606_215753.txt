This was a struggle for me to get through as I typically never listen to political commentary. Not for the fact I don't talk about politics but because I have no interest in focusing on all the drama and going out of my way to get other people's opinions on the politics of the world. I listened to the Countdown with Keith Olbermann podcast which has some very interesting titles like the episode I listened to What if I Think I'm Saving my Country. I kept zoning out while listening and could not get myself to focus much on the podcast itself as it is not something I have an interest in. My reaction was more just me being bored or wanting to fast forward through, I kept having to force myself to sit and listen but kept going off into my own thought processes that were unrelated. This was nothing against the podcast itself, I just couldn't get myself to focus on anything being said and the points I did get myself to listen to just seemed so filled with rage. I believe a lot goes into the production process as they have to do a ton of research into the politics and try to figure out what is real and what is fake. So much fake news goes around when it comes to political talks and often people get very offended fast. They likely have to be careful not to offend their audience as it is a quick way to lose people but, this podcast didn't seem from what I could tell to care about that. Making sure their information is correct also is a huge thing as spreading fake news when it comes to politics and ruining credibility entirely but, I also noticed they don't fully care about that either which amazes me. I wish I could have been able to fully focus on this podcast and actually form opinions on what was being said but it just seemed so filled with rage that my brain tuned it out entirely.
