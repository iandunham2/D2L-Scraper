For my last episode, I am going to talk about the most humble act that Jesus did on Earth, which is sacrificing Himself on the cross for all of our sins. This also perfectly lines up with the timing of Easter so that is great. I am going to include a few friends on this episode to help me discuss this incredible and sacrificial act that we are still walking in the freedom of today. I have yet to decide if I will do video or not. But in terms of preparation I will be recording much earlier in the week so that I can have a few days to edit. Normally I record on Friday or Saturday and edit that day or the next. But if I am doing video I want to make sure I have enough time for learning and perfecting the logistics of that. My favorite podcast I have recorded flowed like a conversation and that is exactly what I want this one to sound like as well. Just friends talking about someone we all love! I also want to make sure I have time to confirm I do everything on the rubric for sound quality and editing requirements. I am excited!
