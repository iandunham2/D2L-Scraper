This week I decided to look at some podcasts that have never really piqued my interest in the past. Usually, I listen to interview-style podcasts and comedy podcasts that talk about things that arent very current and arent really about content. Joe Rogan is a good example of the style that I listen to, more story-based and learning about specific guests that come on the show. I had never listened to a scientific research podcast, same with political commentary, music reviews and dramatized content. I decided that I wanted to listen to a music review podcast because that seemed like something I might be interested in since I love listening to and talking about music. I really enjoy Kendrick Lamars music so I looked up podcasts that might pertain to him or his discography. I stumbled upon this podcast called Dissect, a podcast in which the host listens to songs and albums and dissects the meaning and breaks down the significance of certain songs. The episode I picked was one where the host decided to listen to and dissect Kendrick Lamar's album To Pimp a Butterfly. I really enjoyed listening to this podcast and to be honest I didn't think I would. In the Podcast, the host talks about these songs and breaks down the lyrics and bars of these songs to explain them and realte their relevance to current times, this was impressive because Kendrick Lamar is looked at like a poet of our generation and not everything he says is so easy to analyze or understand. I would say a majority of the time spent on the production of this podcast is content prep and research time. The host seemed to be very knowledgable on the songs talked about and they seemed very fluid in discussion. The host probably did their own research as well as input their own opinions.
