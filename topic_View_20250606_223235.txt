I originally had some troubles finding a podcast that uses music well as most jut plopped it in or didn't even actually have it. However, one of my friends recommended a podcast that actually goes into music production and uses it extremely well. This podcast is known as Tape Notes and I listened to Episode 152. When the podcast begins, they play music at the start and then when introducing the podcast, they keep the music playing but quieter in the background. When mentioning the music they are speaking about, they play a sample of the music in the background quietly to where people can understand the music and when introducing the people they are interviewing, they do play their music as the main audio. It's a perfect introduction and lasts roughly 40 seconds before they go looking right into it and dissecting what went into making the song. Its strategic to slowly introduce before fully introducing so the audience feels more of a connection than just music out of nowhere and having people introduced by the work, they have made develops a sense of respect from the audience and interest to hear what went into making it. While I am working on a serial killer podcast and the topics are vastly different, adding some music as ambiance to get the atmosphere made would likely be very useful.


Link: Best Of Season 9 - Pt 1 - Tape Notes
