Overall, I've loved being in this class and being so free to create something I am so passionate about! At first the thought of creating my own podcast was daunting, but the semester has shown me so far that once you have the process locked in then the only challenging part is choosing a topic to talk about each week. It's hard for me because I have so many ideas for my podcast, but don't have the time to execute all 1,000 things I want to discuss. I think the interview episode was my favorite by far, because it gave me an automatic wall to bounce ideas back and forth with as well as something more interesting to edit an listen to! I am also really excited to be able to review one of my classmates podcasts this week!
