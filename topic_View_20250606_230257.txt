I had never really edited and created a podcast until this class. I did not realize how much I would like it. I feel like I learned a lot of valuable skills in this class like making sure your audio is crisp and loud enough while also adding different sound effects to make the podcast sound better and more immersive. I hope to be a videographer in the future by working for independent companies making independent projects. A lot of indie films do wear different hats where the videographer could also be working on sound or editing the entire thing. I think the skills I learned in here are going to help me with that out there. I have only gotten better and more confident with editing so I can definitely see myself editing some films or content in the future. I also got accepted into the Disney College Program and plan to go down to Orlando in August. I plan to create content while I am down there so I will take the production skills I learned and apply it to that. I know that I did not learn about videography in this class but I think learning about sound quality is just as important. You cannot really make videos if your sound is awful and you don't know how to fix it and make it better. I think a lot of companies are also making sure you are ok to do different roles and I think it is really useful to learn as much as you can about media production. My content in my podcast does correlate to what I want to create so I think it can be very useful to future employers to see what I am interested in. Overall, I think I can take away editing, sound production, and storytelling skills to let future employers know what I am good at.
