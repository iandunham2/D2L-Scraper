 I decided to check out Rotten Mango, a true crime podcast hosted by Stephanie Soo. Each episode is about 45 minutes to over an hour long depending on the complexity of the case being covered. Stephanie narrates these stories solo but her conversational and expressive tone makes it feel like you’re sitting down with a friend. She often dives into grisly or shocking details but she balances it out with moments of humor and personal commentary, making it engaging for listeners who want more than just a straightforward recounting of crimes.
Rotten Mango definitely has a narrative. Each episode walks you through a case step by step starting with the background of the victims and suspects then moving through the events, investigation, and aftermath. Stephanie adds emotional depth by talking about the psychological factors or cultural context surrounding each case which makes it feel more like a story than just a list of facts.
The target audience is likely true crime fans who enjoy a mix of storytelling and personality. It’s not for the faint of heart though since the content can be pretty graphic. As the reading said, “Podcasts thrive on intimacy” (p. 10) and this podcast feels personal because Stephanie shares her reactions and thoughts throughout creating a connection with the audience. If you like a mix of chilling stories and casual commentary this podcast is worth a listen.
Overall Rotten Mango is a gripping and well-told true crime podcast that stands out because of Stephanie Soo’s engaging storytelling and personal touch. It’s perfect for listeners who want an in depth look at chilling cases but also enjoy a conversational almost friend like narration. By blending detailed narratives with her own reactions Stephanie creates a podcast that feels both informative and intimate exemplifying the kind of connection podcasts can build with their audience. If you’re into true crime and don’t mind some dark details this podcast is definitely worth adding to your list.
