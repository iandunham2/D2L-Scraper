This module's reading looked more into personal stories regarding identity and I was drawn to a few of them but Barrack Obama's stood out to me due to how well-known he is. He started off by detailing his early life and how a lack of a father had impacted him significantly. It caused a huge internal struggle in his life as he couldn't figure out who his role model was or even who he was for the longest time and the stereotypes society has didn't help him. Eventually, he realized, that rather than being like others he had to figure out on his own accord who he was and not listen to stereotypes or feel pressured to be someone else. His story alone is very impactful and so is each of the experiences shared.
When watching an episode of WTF, they were talking with Larry King. This episode was something else and was recommended to me by a friend. They talk about a lot of interesting things like him not knowing he had a child from a previous marriage and that his biggest regret is smoking. They also talked about how he got started and what helped make him who he was. The people who had made impressions on him and how he overcame his nervousness. He stated that how it is about control and once you know that the nervousness disappears. There's so much that goes into his identity and the things that made him who he was. His identity shaped so much about his worldview because it allowed him to figure out control in situations. Marc also allowed for him to feel more relaxed and open about everything just building a comfortable atmosphere. Where as King says it, makes the cameras and microphones disappear into the background almost as if they are just having a regular conversation. This makes such a compelling podcast because it feels like we are talking to just another human, hearing their story and the bad and the good.
