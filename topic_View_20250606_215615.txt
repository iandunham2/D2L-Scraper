For this discussion I decided to choose to explore a podcast genre that I have never listened to before which is political commentary. I am typically attracted towards podcasts about pop culture, personal development, or lighthearted conspiracy theories. So the political commentary genre feels a bit intense and heavy for me. Although I did want to give it a try  by listening to an episode of "Pod Save America," a popular political podcast that provides a perspective on current events and political issues.
After listening to the episode I differently had a mix reaction and feelings on it. While I do appreciated the amount of detail and insight that was put into multiple political matters I found the tone to be somewhat confrontational which is common for political commentary podcasts. The hosts were very passionate but the discussions sometimes felt extremely overwhelming especially when it came to covering complicated issues like healthcare or international politics. However I could see how these conversations might be engaging and informative for listeners who are very interested in staying informed about different political events and there development.
In terms of the production I imagine the process had involved a lot of research. The hosts most likely spent time reading up on different current events, reviewing speeches, bills, or interviews, and most likely prepared notes to guide the discussion. They might also looked up the latest polling data or look into the historical context of the topics they cover. The structure of the episode probably involves careful scripting or they at least had to have a solid outline to made sure that they stay on topic while allowing for causal conversation.
The preparation for the episode I feel probably includes a balance of fact-checking but also making sure that all viewpoints are presented in a compelling way as these podcasts are meant to inform and persuade. I also am sure that there was probably also a significant amount of editing to help pacing of the episode. Although this isn’t a genre I typically enjoy it gave me a deeper appreciation for the effort and research that goes into creating a thoughtful political commentary podcast.
