 This podcast was recommended to me by one of my friends who loves drama. The interview focuses on love, dating, and relationships and some episodes will even go into bad first dates. The interview feels very informal but they are typically long around an hour per episode. Nicole Byer makes a huge difference with her informal interviewing style as she builds a nice and comfortable atmosphere for her interviewees making them ready to spill all of the tea. The episode I watched was "How to Hack into Your Partner's Phone" which immediately is a controversial topic to most people by title alone. But it goes into an interview with Keke Palmer and her relationship stories. Truthfully, the most important moment of this interview was Palmer's reflecting upon her prior relationship and what she genuinely learned from it. She gives insightful advice and gives some meaningful messages to people who might be in a similar situation where they had their partner cheat. She goes into detail about the situation but what was most meaningful is how she reflects upon how she handled it and what she genuinely learned from it. Which sometimes that is what someone needs to hear and makes it extremely valuable.

How to Hack Into Your Partner's Phone (w/ Keke Palmer) | Why Won't You Date Me? with Nicole Byer - Podcast on Goodpods
