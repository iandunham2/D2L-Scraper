I chose the episode "What's Up Doc" from the Radiolab podcast. This podcast was released on November 18th, 2022. This episode follows the story of Mel Blanc and talks about all of the voices he played in in characters like Bugs Bunny and Porky Pig. I would say the first thing that really grabbed my attention is when they are introducing his story to the audience, they play sound clips in the background of the character in the background when they are naming the character. This was a perfect example as to how you can get the attention of an audience right off the bat. Editing plays a large role in the constructive of the narrative. They play sound clips of Mel's son in the background as they are introducing segments of him talking in the podcast. They also play music that tenses up the mood and use tension and release sounds and risers when they are talking about the horrible accident that Mel Blanc got into that left him in a lengthy coma. They also play emotional music when they are talking about the whole story of him being in the coma, and how that impacted his family. It seems like you are watching a documentary when you are really just listening. The music plays such a role in being able to create a mental image of what is happening in the podcast. Another interesting segment was when they talked about how Mel woke up from his coma when his son called him "Bugs Bunny". He awoke doing the characters voices he would always do. When they talked about that, they played music that would have been in shows like Bugs Bunny. Overall, the story seems as though it is told in an impromptu manner and it doesn't seem scripted at all. It was a very good podcast episode and kept me totally interested and locked in throughout the whole episode.
