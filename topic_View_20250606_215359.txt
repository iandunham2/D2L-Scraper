Ariana Grande – singer, actress, vegan, Italian, artist

Summary: I chose Ariana Grande and Marc Maron discusses a lot about her identity with her. Within just the first ten minutes of their talk they discussed that Ariana has been a vegan for the past thirteen years which caused some tension with her very Italian household that eats a lot of red meat and cheese. The conversation transitioned to how Ariana has always felt like an artist and a singer and how she’s been singing since she could talk. They delved into how Ariana eventually auditioned for a Broadway play and became an actress. The rest of the conversation involved how she had to have operatic training and acting training for her role as Glinda in Wicked and wrapped up with how she maintains positivity as a celebrity.

Marc’s Interview Style: He seems to have come up with a few topics he wants to venture into before the podcast, but most of his podcast seems like a lot of spontaneous and follow up questions. I could tell for sure that the vegan conversation was unplanned. He also goes into a lot of how he can relate to the subject, which can be for better or ill depending on the circumstance. He’s good at letting the conversation flow into a good areas.

Identity: Ariana being an artist defines a lot of who she is. She’s very hard-working and pays attention to detail like the fact that she knew she would need operatic and acting training for Glinda was very introspective. Though she had years of experience as a singer, she knew that she’d need to push herself if she wanted this role.

This isn’t my favorite kind of podcast, so honestly, it’s not particularly compelling to me. I’m just not one who normally likes to listen to many celebrity interviews anymore. It was interesting to hear about her commitment to the craft, so that was compelling, I guess.
